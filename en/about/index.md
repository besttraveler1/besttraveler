# About us

besttraveler organises group tours abroad and one-day tours in Georgia. The tours are organised by Natali Iashvili.

## What we do

- Flight tours to Europe, Egypt, Asia and Africa — from Tbilisi, Kutaisi or Yerevan.
- Multi-day minibus tours to Turkey and Armenia.
- One-day tours in Georgia — Kakheti, Kazbegi, Truso, Javakheti, David Gareja and more.

## How we work

Every tour has a day-by-day programme planned in advance. On the website you can see what the price includes, what it does not, and what optional excursions cost. On most tours a Georgian-speaking escort travels with the group from Tbilisi.

## Get in touch

Questions about tours, dates or free places — call us or message us on WhatsApp.
