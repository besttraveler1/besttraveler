## Frequently asked questions

**How do I book a tour?**

Call or message us on WhatsApp at +995 579 57 56 36. We confirm a free place and the price, then you hold the place with a deposit. The rest is paid 15 days before the tour.

**How do I pay?**

By bank transfer to our Bank of Georgia or TBC Bank account. We send the account details when you book.

**Are flights included in the price?**

On most flight tours the return flight is included. Each tour page lists exactly what is and is not included.

**Why can the price change?**

Flight tour prices are based on current air fares. Fares go up as the trip gets closer, so it is best to book early.

**Which documents do I need?**

To cross the border you need an international passport valid for at least 6 months. For tours to Armenia and Turkey a Georgian ID card is also accepted. Children need a birth certificate, notarised consent from the parents and a passport.

**Where do the day tours start?**

Day tours start in Tbilisi and go by minibus. The meeting point is usually the upper exit of Didube metro station.

**Do I get my money back if I cannot go?**

If you cannot travel for your own reasons, the money you paid is not returned. See the booking terms for details.
