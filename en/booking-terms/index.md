# Booking terms

How to book a besttraveler tour: deposit, paying the rest, refunds, passport rules and the documents children need to travel abroad.

## Booking and deposit

You hold a place on a tour with a deposit. The deposit amount is shown on each tour page. To confirm a place, contact us by phone or WhatsApp.

## Paying the rest

The rest of the price (minus the deposit) is paid 15 days before the tour starts. Some long-haul tours (for example China) have a different deadline — it is shown on the tour page.

Payment is by bank transfer to our Bank of Georgia or TBC Bank account. We send the account details when you book.

## Price

Flight tour prices are based on current air fares. Fares rise as the departure gets closer, so the price can change — book early and confirm the price before you pay.

## Refunds

- If you cannot travel for your own reasons, the money you paid is not returned.
- The money is not returned if you cannot cross the border because your passport has expired, is not valid long enough, or for another violation.
- On a one-day tour the money is not returned if you cancel in the last two days.

## Documents

- To cross the border you need an international passport valid for at least 6 months.
- For tours to Armenia and Turkey you can travel with a passport or a Georgian ID card.
- Minors need a birth certificate, notarised consent from the parents and an international passport.
- Whether a visa is needed, and its fee, is shown on each tour page (under “Not included”).

## Airlines and weather

- The tour company is not responsible for problems caused by the airline (delays, cancellations, luggage).
- One-day tours run in any weather. A tour is postponed only if there is a problem with the road.
