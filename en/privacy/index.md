# Privacy

besttraveler.ge is an information website about our tours.

## What data we collect

The website does not collect or store personal data. The request form does not send your data anywhere — it only opens WhatsApp with a ready message that you send yourself.

## Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser only remembers the theme you chose (light or dark).

When a tour map is shown, your browser loads map tiles from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). OpenFreeMap does not use cookies.
