## Booking and payment

**How do I book a tour?**

Call or message us on WhatsApp at +995 579 57 56 36. We confirm a free place and the price, then you hold the place with a deposit.

**How big is the deposit?**

Each tour has its own deposit. It is shown on the tour page next to the price.

**When do I pay the rest?**

The rest is paid 15 days before the tour starts. For some long-haul tours (for example China) it is 1 month before; this is shown on the tour page.

**How do I pay?**

By bank transfer to our Bank of Georgia or TBC Bank account. We send the account details when you book.

**Do I get my money back?**

If you cannot travel for your own reasons, the money you paid is not returned. It is also not returned if you cannot cross the border because of an expired or insufficient passport.

**Why can the price change?**

Flight tour prices are based on current air fares. Fares rise as the departure gets closer, so it is best to book early.

## Documents and visas

**Which documents do I need?**

An international passport valid for at least 6 months. For tours to Armenia a Georgian ID card is also accepted.

**What does a child need?**

A birth certificate, notarised consent from the parents and an international passport.

**Do I need a visa?**

Whether a visa is needed, and its fee, is shown on each tour page (under “Not included”). See our visa articles in the blog for details.

## Flight tours

**Are flights included?**

On most flight tours the return flight is included. Each tour page lists exactly what is included.

**Where do we fly from?**

Depending on the tour, from Tbilisi, Kutaisi or Yerevan airport. The airport is shown on the tour page.

**What luggage is included?**

Most tours from Kutaisi include 10 kg hand luggage (40x30x20 cm). Some tours also include checked baggage — see the tour page.

**Who travels with the group?**

On most tours an escort travels with the group from Tbilisi; on some tours there is also a local guide.

## Day tours

**Where do day tours start?**

In Tbilisi, by minibus. The meeting point is usually the upper exit of Didube metro station.

**When do we leave and come back?**

Usually we leave at 06:00–08:00 and come back at 21:00–23:00. The exact time is on the tour page.

**Does the tour run in bad weather?**

Day tours run in any weather. A tour is postponed only if there is a problem with the road.

**Are meals included?**

On day tours you usually take care of food yourself.
