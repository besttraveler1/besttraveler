## Do Georgian citizens need a visa for Jordan?

Yes, but it is easy to get. Jordan's Ministry of Interior splits countries into two groups: citizens of 'restricted' countries need prior approval to enter, citizens of 'non-restricted' countries do not. Georgia is not on the ministry's published list of restricted countries.

According to the official Jordan Pass website, citizens of non-restricted countries get a visa on arrival at Queen Alia International Airport in Amman and at the Sheikh Hussein Bridge (Jordan River) border crossing.

## How much does the Jordan visa cost?

According to the official Jordan Pass website, as of October 2026:

- Single-entry tourist visa without the Jordan Pass: 40 Jordanian dinars (about 60 USD, as the site puts it)
- The single-entry visa is valid for one month and can be extended at a police station for an extra fee

## The Jordan Pass: visa and entry tickets in one

The Jordan Pass is an official tourist pass. It covers entry to more than 40 sites, including Petra, Jerash and Wadi Rum, and it also covers the single-entry tourist visa fee. There are three versions:

- Jordan Wanderer: 70 dinars (one day in Petra)
- Jordan Explorer: 75 dinars (two consecutive days in Petra)
- Jordan Expert: 80 dinars (three consecutive days in Petra)

The visa fee is waived only if you buy the Jordan Pass before you arrive in Jordan and stay at least two full nights (three days). The airport immigration system checks this when you leave. The pass covers a single-entry visa only, not a multiple-entry one.

## Aqaba: a free visa when you arrive by sea

Aqaba is a special economic zone. The official tourism website of the zone's authority (ASEZA) says that visitors entering through Aqaba's port, airport or land border (except the southern Wadi Araba crossing) receive a free visa to Jordan. The condition: you must leave Jordan within one month through the same border.

The same site notes that some nationalities need a visa before travel and that the rules can change without notice, so check again before you go.
