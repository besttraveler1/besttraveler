## What you see in one day

Our Kazbegi tour is a straightforward day trip up the Georgian Military Road. We leave Tbilisi in the morning and are back in the evening. The stops are:

- Dariali Monastery complex, in the Dariali gorge, which ends at the Russian border.
- The big Gveleti waterfall: a walk of 1.2 km one way, medium difficulty.
- The small Gveleti waterfall: an easy walk of about 500 m.
- Gergeti Trinity Church, below Mount Kazbek at about 2,170 m above sea level.
- The Gudauri panorama, a viewpoint on the way back.

Ananuri is not on our Kazbegi itinerary. If seeing it matters to you, ask us before you book.

## How long is the drive?

Stepantsminda, the town people call Kazbegi, is about 157 km north of Tbilisi and sits at around 1,740 m. The road crosses the Jvari Pass, its highest point at 2,379 m. Gudauri is about 120 km from Tbilisi at roughly 2,200 m.

The tour leaves at 07:00 and gets back around 22:00, so it is a full day with a lot of time in the vehicle. Get a good night's sleep and bring water and snacks for the road.

## How hard is the walking?

The longest walk on our tour is to the big Gveleti waterfall: 1.2 km one way, medium difficulty, and the same distance back. The path to the small waterfall is easy, about 500 m. If the big waterfall is too much for you, you can visit only the small one.

You will feel the altitude more than you do in the city. If you have heart or breathing problems, ask your doctor before the trip and tell the tour escort.

## What to wear and bring, by season

Mountain weather changes fast and is cooler than Tbilisi. Dress in layers you can take off or put on easily.

- Summer: light clothes plus a warm top, a rain jacket, sunglasses, a hat and sunscreen.
- Spring and autumn: a warm jacket, a hat and waterproof shoes. Paths can be wet or muddy.
- Winter: a winter coat, gloves, a hat and shoes with non-slip soles. Gudauri is a ski resort whose season runs from December to April, so expect snow.
- Any season: comfortable walking or sports shoes, water, snacks and a power bank.
- For Gergeti Trinity Church: women need a headscarf and a skirt, men long trousers.

## Kazbegi or Truso?

The Kazbegi tour is about views and short walks. The Truso Gorge tour has much more walking: 6-7 km one way on an easy path, past colourful travertines, the 'bottomless' lake, Ketrisi village, Abano Convent and Zakagori Fortress. If you enjoy walking, choose Truso. If you want to see a lot without much effort, choose Kazbegi.
