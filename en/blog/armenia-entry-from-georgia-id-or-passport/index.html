Entering Armenia from Georgia: ID Card or Passport?

Published: 16 Oct 20262 min read
Short answer

Georgian citizens do not need a visa for Armenia. Under an agreement in force since 2023, you can enter Armenia with a biometric ID card through an international border checkpoint, or with your passport. Armenia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs says visa-free foreigners may stay no more than 180 days in a year, unless an international agreement sets a different term.

Do Georgian citizens need a visa for Armenia?

No. Armenia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs keeps an official list of countries with which Armenia has a visa-free regime under bilateral or multilateral agreements. Georgia is on it for all types of passports. Tourists and people visiting family do not need a visa.

Can you enter Armenia with an ID card?

Yes. According to Georgia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the agreement on visa-free travel between Georgia and Armenia entered into force on 13 July 2023. The prime ministers of the two countries signed it in Yerevan on 12 January 2023.

Under the agreement, citizens of Georgia and Armenia can travel between the two countries with a biometric ID card, through a border checkpoint for international travel. It replaced the old agreement of 1993.

Use only a valid biometric (chip) ID card. If you are not sure your card qualifies, take your passport.

Which document to take

  • Biometric ID card: handy for crossing the land border by bus or car.
  • Passport: also accepted. You will need it if you fly on from Armenia to another country, for example from Yerevan airport.

The last point matters: some of our tours to Europe fly from Yerevan airport. On those trips the rules of your destination apply, and for Europe that means a biometric passport.

How long can you stay in Armenia?

The visa page of Armenia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs says that foreign citizens with a visa-free regime may stay in Armenia for no more than 180 days during one year, unless Armenia's international agreements set a different term.

That is more than enough for a holiday. If you plan a long stay, or want to work or study in Armenia, check the rules with the Armenian embassy.

Travelling to Armenia with children

According to Armenia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, foreign minors may enter Armenia with their parents, one parent or another legal representative, or when travelling to join them or a receiving organisation.

A child needs their own valid document. If the child travels without parents or with only one parent, check Georgia's exit rules with the Public Service Hall in advance.

Quick checklist

  1. A valid biometric ID card or passport for every traveller.
  2. Cross at a border checkpoint for international travel.
  3. Flying on from Yerevan: take your passport and check the destination's rules.
  4. For a child: their own document and, if needed, parental consent.

We run day trips and multi-day tours to Armenia from Tbilisi: Dilijan, Lori with Haghpat and Sanahin, Jermuk, Tatev and Noravank, and Yerevan with Garni, Geghard and Etchmiadzin. On all our Armenia tours you can travel with a passport or a Georgian ID card.

Checked against official sources. Rules can change — please confirm before you travel.

Frequently asked questions

Do Georgian citizens need a visa for Armenia?

No. According to Armenia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the visa-free regime with Georgia covers all types of passports.

Can I enter Armenia with a Georgian ID card?

Yes, with a biometric ID card, through a border checkpoint for international travel. The agreement has been in force since July 2023.

How long can a Georgian citizen stay in Armenia?

Under the general rule of Armenia's MFA, no more than 180 days in a year unless an agreement sets a different term. Check with the embassy before a long stay.

Is an ID card enough to fly from Yerevan to Europe?

No. To fly to Europe you need a biometric passport.

Does a child need their own document?

Yes. A child needs their own valid passport or biometric ID card.

Sources

  1. Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Georgia: Agreement between Georgia and the Republic of Armenia on visa-free travel entered into force (13 July 2023)
  2. Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Armenia: Countries with which Armenia has a visa-free regime under bilateral and multilateral agreements
  3. Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Armenia: Visa (length of visa-free stay, entry of minors)
