# Svaneti Guide: What to See in Mestia and Ushguli and How to Get There

Short answerThe highlights of Upper Svaneti are Mestia, with its Svan towers and the Svaneti Museum of History and Ethnography, and Ushguli, part of a UNESCO World Heritage Site. You reach Mestia by road via Zugdidi, or by a small plane from Natakhtari near Tbilisi. Plan at least 3 days.

## Where Svaneti is and why go

Upper Svaneti lies in north-west Georgia, high in the Greater Caucasus along the upper Enguri river. Its main town is Mestia, at about 1,500 m. Upper Svaneti has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1996, mainly for its medieval villages and stone tower houses.

People come for mountain views, old villages and hiking in summer, and for skiing in winter.

## What to see in Mestia

- Svan towers — stone tower houses, most of them built between the 9th and 12th centuries. Many are easy to see from the centre of Mestia.
- Svaneti Museum of History and Ethnography — old manuscripts, icons and traditional Svan clothing.
- Chalaadi glacier — a fairly easy walk from Mestia through forest and across streams to the ice.
- Koruldi lakes — a longer hike with views of the Caucasus peaks. You need to be reasonably fit.

## Ushguli

Ushguli is the highest village in Svaneti, about 46 km from Mestia. It is a group of small villages (Zhibiani, Chvibiani, Chazhashi, Murqmeli and Lamjurishi) at about 2,100 m, at the foot of Mount Shkhara. You see old towers and the Lamaria church on the hill above Zhibiani.

The road to Ushguli is a mountain road and depends on the weather. In winter and after heavy rain it can be hard going or closed for a while. Check road conditions before you set off.

## How to get to Svaneti

- From Tbilisi by road — via Kutaisi and Zugdidi. Georgia's official tourism site puts the drive at about 8-10 hours.
- From Kutaisi — about 4-5 hours by road, according to the same source.
- From Batumi — up the coast to Zugdidi, then the mountain road to Mestia.
- From Zugdidi — Mestia is about 128 km north-east of Zugdidi. There is also a train from Tbilisi to Zugdidi.
- By air — Mestia has Queen Tamar Airport. Small planes fly from Natakhtari (near Tbilisi) and from Kutaisi, but not every day, and flights are often moved because of mountain weather.

## When to go

For hiking and for Ushguli, the warm months are best: summer and early autumn, when roads and trails are easiest to use. In winter Svaneti is a ski area: Hatsvali and Tetnuldi are close to Mestia. The season usually opens at the end of December, and slopes open step by step as snow allows.

## How many days you need

The trip is long, so Svaneti does not work as a day trip. Plan at least 3 days: one for the drive and Mestia, one for Ushguli, one to get back. The official tourism site suggests 3-4 days in Mestia. Add more days if you want to hike.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is there to see in Svaneti?**

In Mestia: the Svan towers and the Svaneti Museum of History and Ethnography, with the Chalaadi glacier nearby. About 46 km away is Ushguli, at the foot of Mount Shkhara.

**How do I get to Mestia from Batumi?**

Drive up the coast to Zugdidi, then take the mountain road to Mestia. You can also join our group tour.

**How long is the drive from Tbilisi to Mestia?**

About 8-10 hours, according to Georgia's official tourism site. Flying is faster, but flights do not run every day.

**How many days do I need in Svaneti?**

At least 3 days to see Mestia and Ushguli without rushing. Allow more if you want to hike.

**Can you visit Ushguli in winter?**

Ushguli is under snow for a large part of the year and the road can close in winter. Check conditions before you go.

**Is there skiing in Svaneti?**

Yes. Hatsvali and Tetnuldi ski areas are near Mestia. The season usually opens at the end of December.
