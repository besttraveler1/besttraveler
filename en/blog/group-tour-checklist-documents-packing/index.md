## Documents

- Passport: our booking terms require it to be valid for at least 6 months after the trip ends. Check that you have blank pages too.
- A copy or photo of your passport, on your phone and on paper, kept separately.
- Travel insurance: included in most of our flight tours. Save the policy on your phone.
- Tickets and hotel details: the tour escort handles these, but keep your own copy.
- A visa, if the country requires one: check the entry rules with an official source or the embassy.

## Money and cards

- Two cards from different banks, so you have a backup if one gets blocked.
- Tell your bank you're travelling and check your limits.
- Some cash in local currency for small costs, entry tickets and toilets.
- Don't keep all your money in one place.

## Hand luggage on low-cost flights from Kutaisi

Many of our tours from Kutaisi (for example Athens, Vilnius, Bratislava and Brussels) include 10 kg of hand luggage, 40x30x20 cm. That is a small bag, so plan ahead:

- Weigh and measure your bag at home. Extra weight or size costs a lot at the airport.
- Wear your heaviest shoes and jacket instead of packing them.
- Pack clothes that mix and match, and take small toiletry tubes.
- Pack liquids and cosmetics according to the airport's rules.
- Baggage rules can change, so check the exact terms on your ticket.

## Electronics

- Adapter: check the socket type at your destination. In some countries a Georgian plug won't fit.
- Charger and power bank (power banks go in hand luggage only).
- Mobile data: roaming, a local SIM or an eSIM.

## Medicines and health

- Enough of your regular medicines for the whole trip, in their original packaging.
- A prescription or doctor's note if you take strong or unusual medicines.
- A small first-aid kit: painkillers, something for the stomach, plasters and, if you need it, travel-sickness tablets.
- Keep medicines in your hand luggage, not in checked baggage.

## Children: what documents they need

Under Article 8 of the Law of Georgia on the Procedure for Georgian Citizens Leaving and Entering Georgia, a child under 16 may leave Georgia temporarily with the permission of one legal representative (a parent) and must be accompanied by an adult with full legal capacity. A child aged 16-18 who is not in a registered marriage may leave with the consent of one legal representative. If the parents disagree, a court decides.

- The child's own passport.
- Birth certificate, to show that you are the parent.
- If the child travels without a parent (for example with a grandparent or relative), a written consent from a parent certified by a notary. According to the Georgian Notary Chamber, a parent who is abroad can also have the consent certified online.
- Copies of the passport and the consent, kept separately.

The destination country may have its own rules for minors. Before travelling with a child, confirm the rules with the border police, a notary or the country's embassy (as of October 2026).

## What to do next

Choose a tour and check your passport before booking. If you're travelling with a child, prepare the consent early. You can ask us any questions when you book.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long must my passport be valid?**

Our booking terms require at least 6 months after the trip ends.

**How much hand luggage is included from Kutaisi?**

On our Kutaisi tours, 10 kg of hand luggage, 40x30x20 cm. Check your ticket as well.

**Is insurance included?**

Travel insurance is included in most of our flight tours. See the 'included' list on the tour page.

**Does my child need the other parent's consent?**

Under Georgian law, a child under 16 needs one legal representative's permission and an adult escort. If the child travels without a parent, bring a notarised consent and confirm the details in advance.

**Which medicines should I take?**

Your regular medicines for the whole trip, in their original packaging, in your hand luggage.
