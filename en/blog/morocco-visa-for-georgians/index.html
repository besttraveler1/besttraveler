Morocco Visa for Georgian Citizens: eVisa or Embassy Visa (2026)

Published: 17 Oct 20262 min read
Short answer

Yes, Georgian citizens need a visa for Morocco. According to Morocco's official portal (acces-maroc.ma), a Georgian can get an eVisa only if they hold a valid multiple-entry visa from the Schengen area, the USA, the UK or another listed country; otherwise the visa is issued through an embassy or consulate. The Morocco visa is included in the price of our Morocco tour.

Do Georgian citizens need a visa for Morocco?

Yes. The official portal of Morocco's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, acces-maroc.ma, has a form called 'Do I need a visa to enter Morocco?'. On 17 October 2026 we filled it in for a Georgian citizen living in Georgia, travelling on an ordinary passport for tourism.

The answer depends on whether the traveller holds another country's valid visa:

  • No other visa: the portal points you to a regular visa issued through an embassy or consulate
  • A valid visa from one of the listed countries: you can apply for an eVisa online

Who can get the eVisa

According to the portal, a Georgian citizen can get an eVisa if they hold one of these visas:

  • a Schengen visa
  • a UK, USA or Ireland visa
  • a Canada, Australia or New Zealand visa
  • a Japan or Norway visa

That visa must be multiple-entry and valid for more than 90 days.

Georgian citizens travel to the Schengen area without a visa, so many of them do not have a Schengen visa. In that case the eVisa is not available and you need a regular visa.

eVisa fee and processing time

As of October 2026 the portal offers two eVisa options:

  • Standard: 770 Moroccan dirhams, processed in 3 to 7 business days
  • Express: 1100 Moroccan dirhams, processed in 1 to 3 business days

The eVisa is single-entry and allows a stay of up to 30 days. The fee for a refused application is not refunded.

What you confirm in the application

In the eVisa application the traveller confirms that they:

  • are travelling for tourism
  • have a return ticket
  • have a hotel booking or an invitation
  • have enough money, at least 70 EUR per day
  • will still hold a valid passport when leaving Morocco

The portal also checks that your passport is valid for more than 90 days. For our tours, your passport must be valid for at least 6 months.

If you do not have another country's visa

In this case the portal tells you to contact a Moroccan embassy or consulate and book an appointment. Check the list of documents and the processing times on the consular portal consulat.ma or with the embassy directly, as they can change.

Morocco with us: the visa is included

The Morocco visa is included in the price of our 10-day group tour of Morocco with a stop in Rome. The programme covers Rome, Marrakesh, Ait Ben Haddou, Rabat, Chefchaouen, Fes and Casablanca. When you book, our manager will tell you which documents to bring for the visa and by what date.

Visa processing takes time, so book early and get your documents ready in good time.
