Short answerWinter in Georgia is mild in the lowlands: Tbilisi's average January high is about 6-7 °C, and Batumi's January average is about 5 °C. The mountains get plenty of snow, and ski resorts such as Gudauri, Bakuriani, Mestia and Goderdzi usually run from late December to April. Cities, museums and wine regions stay open all winter.

## What winter is like in Georgia

The Greater Caucasus range blocks much of the cold air from the north, so winters in the lowlands are fairly mild. The weather differs a lot from one region to another.

- Tbilisi and the east — in the 1991-2020 normals, Tbilisi's average January high is 6.6 °C and the average low is −0.8 °C. Snow is not frequent and rarely stays long.
- Black Sea coast — Batumi's average January temperature is about 5.4 °C. Winters are damp and rainy; snow is rare.
- Mountains — lots of snow higher up. Some mountain villages are hard to reach in winter; Ushguli, for example, is under snow for a large part of the year.

## Where to ski

- Gudauri — at about 2,200 m, roughly 120 km from Tbilisi. The official tourism site gives the season as late December to late April, with January and February the best months.
- Bakuriani — at about 1,700 m in the Borjomi gorge. The gentle Didveli slopes are recommended for beginners and families. The narrow-gauge 'Kukushka' train links Borjomi and Bakuriani when it runs.
- Mestia (Svaneti) — Hatsvali, about 8 km from Mestia, and Tetnuldi, home to the longest ski run in the South Caucasus.
- Goderdzi (highland Adjara) — at about 2,000 m. It is the closest ski area to Batumi.

In the 2025-2026 season, Kokhta in Bakuriani opened on 20 December and lifts at the other resorts started on 27 December. Slopes open step by step as the snow allows, so exact dates change every year.

## Kazbegi in winter

In winter, Stepantsminda and Gergeti Trinity Church sit under snow, and the views are beautiful. The road goes over the Jvari Pass and through Gudauri. Our winter day tour combines Ananuri, Gudauri, Stepantsminda and Gergeti Trinity Church.

In winter the Gudauri-Kobi section of the road can close to all vehicles for a while because of avalanche risk, as announced by Georgia's Roads Department. When that happens, the Kazbegi tour may be moved or changed. The Upper Lars border crossing depends on the same road.

## Tbilisi in winter

Winter is a good time to explore Tbilisi: the old town, Narikala, museums and restaurants. In Abanotubani, below Narikala, are the famous sulphur baths, and a warm bath on a cold day is one of the nicest parts of a winter trip. The baths are part of our Tbilisi city tour.

## What still works in winter

- Cities — Tbilisi, Kutaisi, Batumi.
- Mtskheta and Uplistsikhe, Kakheti and its wine cellars — they are in the lowlands, so you can visit them in winter too.
- Ski resorts and Borjomi park.
- High mountain trails, some passes and remote villages are often closed or hard to reach.

## What to wear

For Tbilisi, a warm jacket is usually enough. For the mountains, bring a winter jacket, hat, gloves and shoes with non-slip soles. The mountain sun is strong, so pack sunglasses and sun cream too.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the weather like in Georgia in winter?**

Mild in the lowlands: Tbilisi's average January high is about 6-7 °C and Batumi's January average is about 5 °C. The mountains are cold and snowy.

**When does the ski season start in Gudauri?**

Usually at the end of December. In 2025-2026 the lifts started on 27 December. The exact date depends on the snow each year.

**Which ski resort is best for children?**

The official tourism site recommends the gentle Didveli slopes in Bakuriani for families and beginners.

**Can you visit Kazbegi in winter?**

Yes, but the Gudauri-Kobi section sometimes closes because of avalanche risk. Check the road before you set off.

**What is there to do in Georgia in winter?**

Tbilisi and its sulphur baths, snowy Kazbegi, skiing in Gudauri, Bakuriani or Svaneti, and wine cellars in Kakheti.
