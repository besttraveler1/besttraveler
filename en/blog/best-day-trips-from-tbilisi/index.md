# Best Day Trips from Tbilisi: Where to Go and Who Each Suits

Published: 16 Oct 20263 min read

Photo: [Bernard Gagnon](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ananuri_Fortress_02.jpg), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Short answerFor little walking, pick Kakheti, the colourful desert or Kazbegi. If you like hiking, pick Truso (6-7 km one way, easy) or Lagodekhi (8.3 km one way, medium). Go to the mountain routes in the warm months. Kakheti and the lowland routes work for most of the year.

## How to choose

Our day tours leave Tbilisi in the morning (06:00-08:00) and come back in the evening, by minibus with a tour escort. Two things matter most when choosing: how much walking you want and what time of year it is.

## Light walking: history and views

- Kakheti, Sighnaghi and Bodbe: Ninotsminda, Manavi Fortress, Bodbe Convent of St Nino and Sighnaghi. Good for families and older travellers.
- Classic Kakheti: Ujarma, Gombori Pass, Shuamta, Ikalto, Tetri Giorgi and Alaverdi.
- Other Kakheti routes: Nekresi, Ilia Lake, Gremi and Telavi, or Chailuri, Gurjaani Kvelatsminda, Zegaani and Sanagire.
- Kojori and Manglisi: close to the city, with fortresses and churches, ending at the Didgori battle memorial.
- Tianeti: Mamkoda, a bear shelter, Ertso fortress and Sioni Lake.
- Kvemo Kartli: Tserakvi, Gagi Fortress and the Tamarisi lakes.

The Kakheti, Kojori-Manglisi and Dzama tours run in any weather. They are postponed only if there is a problem with the road.

## Desert and easy walks

The colourful desert and David Gareja tour covers Dodo's Rka, the David Gareja monastery complex, the Mravaltskaro colourful mountains and reservoir. Walking is easy, about 1 km in total. If you only want to see the cave monasteries, there is also a Sabereebi tour.

There is little shade in the desert. On warm days bring water, a hat and sunscreen.

## Mountains and waterfalls

- Kazbegi: Dariali, the Gveleti waterfalls, Gergeti Trinity Church and the Gudauri panorama. 1.2 km one way to the big waterfall (medium) and 500 m to the small one (easy). Stepantsminda is about 157 km from Tbilisi.
- Khada Gorge: the confluence of the White and Black Aragvi and the Tskere and Rostiani waterfalls. A 4.2 km walk one way, easy.
- Truso Gorge: colourful travertines, the 'bottomless' lake, Abano Convent and Zakagori Fortress. 6-7 km one way, easy but long.
- Lagodekhi: up the Ninoskhevi gorge to the Gurgeniani waterfall. 8.3 km one way, medium and hard in places. This is the longest trail among our day tours.

## Lakes and the high plateau

- Javakheti lakes: Paravani (Georgia's largest lake), the Poka monastery and convent, Saghamo Lake, Gorelovka and the Madatapa reserve.
- Tabatskuri: over the Tskhratskaro Pass (2,462 m) to Tabatskuri village (2,000 m), with free time at the lake and Timotesubani Monastery. This one leaves at 06:00.
- Samtskhe-Javakheti: Zarzma, Chule, Akhaltsikhe's Rabati and Sapara, with guide Giorgi Beridze-Berjavakhi.

## Which season?

On the high mountain routes (Kazbegi, Truso, Tabatskuri, Javakheti), weather and road conditions decide a lot. Winter there means snow and frost, so plan these for the warm months. Gudauri's ski season, for example, runs from December to April. Kakheti, Gareja, Kojori and Kvemo Kartli are easy to visit at different times of year. The desert gets hot in summer, so spring and autumn are more comfortable there.

Exact dates and prices are on each tour page. If you have a health condition, ask us how much walking a tour involves before you book.

## What to do next

Pick one or two routes, check the tour page and book a seat. If you can't decide, write to us and tell us how much you can walk and who you are travelling with.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which day trip is the easiest?**

The Kakheti routes, Kojori-Manglisi and the colourful desert, where walking is about 1 km in total and easy.

**Which one is for hikers?**

Truso (6-7 km one way, easy) and Lagodekhi (8.3 km one way, medium and hard in places).

**What time do the tours leave?**

Most leave at 07:00 or 08:00. Tabatskuri leaves at 06:00. The exact time is on each tour page.

**Can I go to Kazbegi or Truso in winter?**

The mountains get snow in winter and road conditions change. Plan mountain routes for the warm months and check winter dates with us.

**Where should I go with children?**

The easiest options are the Kakheti routes, Kojori-Manglisi, Tianeti and the colourful desert.
