# Entering Georgia in 2026: visa-free stay, insurance, passport

Short answerSince 1 January 2026 every foreign tourist entering Georgia must hold health and accident insurance of at least 30,000 GEL that covers the whole stay. Citizens of 96 countries, including the EU, the USA, the UK, Russia, Belarus and Kazakhstan, need no visa and may stay up to one full year. This is the situation as of October 2026.

This guide is for foreign visitors and for anyone inviting friends or family to Georgia. Every rule below comes from the consular website of Georgia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs or from the official legal database, Matsne.

## Mandatory travel insurance from 2026

Georgia's Law on Tourism says tourists visiting Georgia must have mandatory health and accident insurance. The details are set by Government Decree No 602 of 26 December 2025, which applies from 1 January 2026.

- Sum insured: at least 30,000 GEL.
- The insurer can be Georgian or foreign.
- The policy must cover your whole stay, from the day you enter until you leave, including arrival and departure days.
- Paper or electronic, in Georgian or English.
- At the border, an officer of the Patrol Police Department of the Ministry of Internal Affairs may check that you hold a policy.

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs lists what the policy must show: the parties, the covered territory, what is insured, start and end dates, the insured risks, the sum insured (limits), and the premium with its place and terms of payment. Many standard travel policies meet this, but check the amount and the dates.

Exempt are holders of diplomatic or special visas; holders of diplomatic, official, service or special passports; accredited diplomats and staff of international organisations with their accredited family members; people entering under international agreements of Georgia; and drivers in international road transport.

The decree does not set a fine. However, the Law on the Legal Status of Aliens says a foreigner may be refused entry without health and accident insurance. We found no official source saying you can buy a policy at the border, so buy it before you travel. We are a travel agency and do not sell insurance.

## Who can enter without a visa

Government Decree No 255 lists the countries whose citizens may enter Georgia without a visa and stay for a full year. The list includes all EU countries, Switzerland, Norway, the UK, the USA, Canada, Australia, Japan, Israel, Turkey, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Moldova and others.

- Russia: no visa, up to 1 full year. The ministry's "Do I need a visa?" tool shows the same.
- Ukraine: no visa, up to 1 year. Ukrainians who entered before 24 February 2025 may stay until 24 February 2027.
- China: no visa, but only for 30 days and only for tourism.
- Citizens of the EU, Switzerland and Liechtenstein may also enter with a national ID card.

If your country is not on the list, you may still enter without a Georgian visa for 90 days in any 180 days if you hold a valid visa or residence permit of the USA, the UK, an EU or Schengen country or another listed country. Everyone else needs a visa; a short-term visa can be requested online at evisa.gov.ge, and the ministry says a decision on such an application takes up to 5 working days.

## Passport validity

Georgian law asks for a valid travel document. We did not find an official Georgian rule that the passport must be valid for a set number of months after your trip. Our advice: make sure it is valid for the whole stay, and check with your airline, as some ask for more.

## Children

The insurance decree has no exemption for children, so each child needs their own policy and their own valid travel document. If a child travels with one parent or with another adult, carry the birth certificate and a consent letter from the other parent. We did not find a separate Georgian rule on this, but your home country may ask for it when you leave.

## Quick checklist

- Check if you need a visa on geoconsul.gov.ge (Entering Georgia).
- Make sure your passport is valid for the whole trip.
- Buy insurance for everyone, children too: at least 30,000 GEL, for every day of the stay.
- Keep the policy on your phone and, better, on paper too.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need travel insurance to enter Georgia in 2026?**

Yes. Since 1 January 2026 every tourist must hold health and accident insurance of at least 30,000 GEL covering the whole stay.

**Is a policy from a foreign insurer accepted?**

Yes. The policy can be from a Georgian or foreign insurer, in Georgian or English, on paper or electronic.

**Do Russian citizens need a visa for Georgia?**

No. Citizens of Russia may enter without a visa and stay up to one full year. The insurance rule applies to them too.

**How long can I stay in Georgia without a visa?**

Citizens of the listed countries can stay up to one full year. Citizens of China can stay 30 days for tourism.

**How long must my passport be valid?**

The law asks for a valid travel document. We found no official rule with a number of months, so keep it valid for the whole trip and check with your airline.

**Do children need insurance?**

Yes. The decree has no exemption for children, so each child needs their own policy.
