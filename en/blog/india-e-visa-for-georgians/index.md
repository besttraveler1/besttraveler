## Do Georgian citizens need a visa for India?

Yes. Georgian citizens cannot get a visa on arrival: according to the official portal, that option is only for citizens of Japan, South Korea and, under a condition, the UAE. Georgians can apply for an e-Visa on the official portal, indianvisaonline.gov.in. Georgia is on the portal's list of eligible countries.

## Types of e-Tourist Visa

- 30-day e-Tourist Visa: valid for 30 days from your first arrival in India, multiple entry, cannot be extended or converted
- 1-year e-Tourist Visa: valid for 365 days from the date it is granted, multiple entry
- 5-year e-Tourist Visa: valid for five years from the date it is granted, multiple entry

With the 1-year and 5-year visas you cannot stay in India for more than 180 days in total in one calendar year. The portal also offers business, medical, conference and other e-Visas.

## How much does the India visa cost for Georgians?

The e-Visa fee depends on your nationality. As of October 2026, the official fee table lists these amounts for Georgia:

- 30-day e-Tourist Visa (April to June): 10 USD
- 30-day e-Tourist Visa (July to March): 25 USD
- 1-year e-Tourist Visa: 40 USD
- 5-year e-Tourist Visa: 200 USD

A bank charge of 3% is added. The fee is not refunded, even if the visa is refused. The portal stresses that the Government of India charges no extra fee for an urgent visa and that nothing more is paid at the airport.

## When to apply

Apply for the e-Tourist Visa at least 4 days before your arrival and no earlier than 120 days before your trip. The fee must also be paid at least 4 days before travel, or the application will not be processed. The decision comes by email, and you can check the status on the portal.

## Documents you need

- A passport valid for at least 6 months on the day you apply
- A recent photo on a white background: JPEG, square, 10 KB to 1 MB, no glasses
- A scan of your passport's photo page in PDF
- A return or onward ticket and enough money for your stay

Every traveller, including children, needs their own passport and their own application. Your fingerprints and other biometric data are taken at immigration when you arrive in India.

## Where you can enter on an e-Visa

With an e-Visa you can enter India only through designated airports (Delhi is one of them) and seaports, not by land. You can leave through any immigration check post.

Before you travel, make sure your application status on the portal shows 'GRANTED', and carry a copy of the approval. Pay only on the official portal, indianvisaonline.gov.in.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do Georgian citizens need a visa for India?**

Yes. Georgians can get an e-Visa on the official portal, indianvisaonline.gov.in.

**How much is the India tourist visa?**

As of October 2026 the 30-day visa costs 25 USD from July to March and 10 USD from April to June; the 1-year visa is 40 USD and the 5-year visa 200 USD. A 3% bank charge is added.

**How early should I apply?**

At least 4 days before arrival and no earlier than 120 days before.

**Can I get a visa at Delhi airport?**

No. Visa on arrival is not available to Georgian citizens; get the e-Visa in advance.

**How long must my passport be valid?**

At least 6 months on the day you apply.

Yes, each traveller needs their own passport and their own application.
