Hand Luggage on Low-Cost Flights from Kutaisi: What Fits and What Doesn't

Published: 17 Oct 20264 min read
Short answer

Most of our Kutaisi tours include one piece of hand luggage: 10 kg, 40x30x20 cm. That's the size of a small backpack, not a cabin trolley. Pack liquids in containers of up to 100 ml in one clear 1-litre bag, and keep your power bank in your hand luggage or on you.

How big is 40x30x20 cm?

Think of the backpack you carry around town. A wheeled cabin suitcase usually won't fit these measurements. Everything that sticks out counts: outer pockets, handles and wheels.

  • Measure the bag when it's full: soft backpacks bulge once packed.
  • Weigh it at home. A luggage scale is cheap and saves trouble at the airport.
  • Choose a light bag: a heavy one eats into your 10 kg.

The exact terms are on your ticket. Airline rules change, and if your bag is too big or too heavy you may be charged at the airport. If you need more luggage, tell us when you book.

What fits for a 4-5 day trip

A bag this size holds what you need for 4-5 days if you leave the extras at home:

  • 2-3 T-shirts or shirts and one warm layer that go together;
  • one pair of trousers or a skirt in the bag, the other on you;
  • underwear and socks for each day;
  • a light jacket or an umbrella, depending on the season;
  • a small pouch for documents, phone and charger;
  • medicines in their original packaging.

Roll your clothes rather than folding them; they take less space. Stuff socks into your shoes so no space is wasted. Wear your heaviest shoes and jacket on the journey.

Liquids: the 100 ml rule

According to the European Commission, at EU airports you can take liquids, aerosols and gels in your hand luggage only like this:

  • each container holds no more than 100 ml;
  • all of them fit into one transparent, resealable 1-litre plastic bag;
  • medicines and special dietary items you need during the trip, including baby food, are exempt;
  • duty-free liquids bought at the airport or on board are also exempt if they stay in a sealed security bag with the receipt inside.

Shampoo, cream, lotion, toothpaste and perfume all count as liquids. When you fly out of Kutaisi, screening is done by Kutaisi airport's security service; on the way back it's a European airport, where the rules above apply. So it's simplest to pack your liquids this way for the whole trip. Put the bag on top so you can take it out quickly at security.

Wine, oil or any other liquid in a container over 100 ml won't get through security in hand luggage. If you only have hand luggage, buy such souvenirs in the airport duty-free shop or add checked baggage in advance.

Power banks and electronics

Under a May 2025 bulletin from the EU Aviation Safety Agency (EASA), a power bank counts as a spare battery and is not allowed in checked baggage. Keep it on you or in your hand luggage.

  • Protect it from short circuits: keep it in its original packaging or a separate pouch.
  • Pack it away from perfume and other flammable items.
  • The bulletin asks airlines to tell passengers that power banks must not be used to charge devices during the flight.
  • Check the capacity (Wh) printed on it. The bulletin gives 100 Wh for electrically powered equipment, or 160 Wh with the airline's approval. Your airline may be stricter.
  • E-cigarettes also go only in hand luggage or on you.

If the cabin is full and your bag has to go in the hold at the aircraft door, take your power bank and spare batteries out and keep them with you.

What not to pack in hand luggage

  • Knives, large scissors, tools and other sharp or heavy items: these are generally not allowed in the cabin. Check the exact list on the airport's or airline's website.
  • Liquids in containers over 100 ml, including a full water bottle.
  • Spare shoes and 'just in case' items: they take up the most space.
  • Valuables you won't need on the trip.

When 10 kg isn't enough

Some of our tours include checked baggage. For example, our Egypt tours from Tbilisi include 20 kg of baggage, and the Sicily tour includes 20 kg checked baggage on top of 10 kg hand luggage. If you need more luggage on a Kutaisi tour, ask us when booking whether it can be added in advance.

What to do next

Choose a tour, check the 'included' list to see what luggage you get, then pack, measure and weigh your bag at home. Our general list of documents and items is in 'Group Tour Checklist: Documents and Packing for Travel from Georgia'.

Frequently asked questions

How much hand luggage is included on tours from Kutaisi?

On most of our tours, one bag of 10 kg, 40x30x20 cm. Check the tour page and your ticket for the exact terms.

Can I take a wheeled suitcase?

A standard cabin trolley usually doesn't fit 40x30x20 cm. If you need a bigger bag, ask us about extra luggage when you book.

How much liquid can I take in hand luggage?

Each container up to 100 ml, all together in one clear 1-litre bag. Medicines and baby food are exempt.

Where do I pack my power bank?

Only in your hand luggage or on you. Power banks are not allowed in checked baggage.

Can I bring back a bottle of wine?

Not in hand luggage, unless you bought it in the airport duty-free shop and it stays in the sealed bag. Otherwise you'll need checked baggage.

Sources

  1. European Commission (DG MOVE): Liquids, aerosols and gels in cabin baggage
  2. EASA Safety Information Bulletin 2025-03: Passenger and Crew Awareness on the Risks of Lithium Batteries (27 May 2025)
