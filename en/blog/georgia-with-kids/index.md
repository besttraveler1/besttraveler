Short answerChildren usually love Sataplia with its dinosaur footprints, Prometheus Cave, the Batumi Botanical Garden and the sea, Borjomi park and Bakuriani. Choose tours with short walks, pack water, snacks and a warm layer, and remember that mountain day tours take the whole day.

## What works for families

With children, what matters is short walks, something fun at each stop and not too many hours on the road. Below are places families usually enjoy, and the tours of ours that include them.

## Kutaisi and the caves

- Sataplia — a reserve about 10 km from Kutaisi, with dinosaur footprints, a cave and a glass viewing platform over the cliff. For many children this is the highlight of the trip.
- Prometheus Cave — a large cave with stalactites. About 1.8 km is open to visitors, so children will need to walk. It is cool inside; bring a jacket.
- Martvili and Okatse canyons — paths and views over water. Hold small children's hands on the trails.

Our Kutaisi caves and canyons tour covers these places, and from Batumi there is our Prometheus Cave, Sataplia and Kutaisi trip.

## Batumi and the sea

In summer many families choose Batumi: the sea, the boulevard and the Botanical Garden on the Green Cape. The warmest months in Batumi are July and August, with an average temperature of about 23 °C. Our Batumi city tour combines the boulevard and the Botanical Garden.

## Borjomi and Bakuriani

Borjomi Central Park is an easy place for a walk, with mineral springs. The small 'Kukushka' train climbs from Borjomi to Bakuriani when it runs. In winter, the official tourism site recommends Bakuriani's gentle Didveli slopes for beginners and families.

## Tbilisi and Kazbegi

The Tbilisi tour is on foot through the old town, Narikala and Abanotubani. With small children, a stroller is not always easy on the old town streets.

The Kazbegi tour leaves at 07:00 and is back at about 22:00. The path to the small Gveleti waterfall is easy, about 500 m; the big waterfall is 1.2 km one way and of medium difficulty. If your child gets tired, you can skip the big waterfall.

## Practical tips

- Pick a tour whose travel time suits your child's age. Mountain tours are long.
- Pack water, snacks, wet wipes and a toy or book for the road.
- Dress in layers: mountains and caves are cool, the lowlands are hot in summer.
- The mountain sun is strong: hat, sunglasses, sun cream.
- If your child gets car sick, ask a doctor beforehand and tell us, so we can try to seat you near the front.
- For trips abroad, see our Booking terms page for documents for minors.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where to go in Georgia with kids?**

Sataplia for the dinosaur footprints, Prometheus Cave, Batumi for the sea and the Botanical Garden, Borjomi and Bakuriani.

**Which tour is best for small children?**

Tours with short walks: city tours, Sataplia, Borjomi park. The Kazbegi tour takes the whole day and can be tiring for little ones.

**How much walking is there in Prometheus Cave?**

About 1.8 km is open to visitors. Bring a jacket, it is cool inside.

**When is the best time for Batumi with children?**

July and August are the warmest months for the sea. September and October are the wettest months in Batumi.

**Can children ski in Georgia?**

Yes. Bakuriani's gentle Didveli slopes are recommended for beginners and families.
