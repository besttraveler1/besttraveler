Short answerGeorgian citizens need an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) for Sri Lanka, obtained on eta.gov.lk before arrival: a 30-day tourist ETA costs 50 USD in advance, 60 USD on arrival, and is free for children under 12 (as of October 2026). The Maldives grants a tourist visa on arrival free of charge, but you must submit an online Traveller Declaration within 96 hours before you arrive.

## Do Georgian citizens need a visa for Sri Lanka?

Yes. For a short visit to Sri Lanka you need an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA), issued by the Department of Immigration and Emigration on the official site eta.gov.lk. The site says that all foreign nationals must obtain an ETA before arriving in Sri Lanka.

From 25 May 2026 citizens of 40 countries get the tourist ETA free of charge. Georgia is not on that list, so Georgians pay the ETA fee.

## How much is the Sri Lanka ETA for Georgians?

According to the official site, as of October 2026 the fees for 'all other countries' are:

- Tourist ETA obtained in advance (online, at a Sri Lankan mission or at the department's head office): 50 USD
- Tourist ETA on arrival: 60 USD
- Business ETA: 55 USD
- Transit up to 2 days: free
- Children under 12: tourist ETA free for up to 30 days

Online payment is by Visa, Mastercard or American Express only. The fee is not refunded.

## What the ETA gives you and how long it lasts

- The tourist ETA is for 30 days with double entry: you can enter a second time within 30 days of your first arrival
- You must enter Sri Lanka within three months of the date the ETA is issued
- Your stay can be extended up to six months through the immigration department's online service after you arrive
- The tourist ETA does not cover work, journalism or NGO activities

## What the border officer will ask for

- A passport valid for at least 6 months from the date of arrival
- A confirmed return ticket
- Enough money for your stay
- A copy of your ETA approval (the site advises carrying one)

## Maldives: tourist visa on arrival

According to the official Maldives Immigration website, the tourist visa is granted on arrival and no prior approval is needed. You must still meet the basic entry requirements:

- A passport with a machine-readable zone (MRZ), valid for at least one month
- A full itinerary: a confirmed return ticket and a prepaid confirmed booking at a registered facility
- Enough money for your stay
- A yellow fever vaccination certificate if you arrive from a country with a risk of yellow fever

Every foreigner must submit a Traveller Declaration on the IMUGA portal (imuga.immigration.gov.mv) within 96 hours before arriving in the Maldives. It is free. Each traveller, including children, needs their own declaration.
