Short answerNo, Georgian citizens do not need a visa for a short trip to China. Since 28 May 2024 a mutual visa exemption agreement for ordinary passport holders has been in force: each stay must not exceed 30 days, and the total must not exceed 90 days in any 180-day period. Hong Kong has its own rules: Georgians can stay there visa-free for up to 30 days (as of October 2026).

## Do Georgian citizens need a visa for China?

Not for a short visit. On 10 April 2024 the governments of Georgia and China signed an agreement on mutual visa exemption for holders of ordinary passports. According to the official notice of the Chinese Embassy in Georgia, the agreement took effect at 00:00 Beijing time on 28 May 2024.

## The exact visa-free terms

According to the embassy notice, citizens of both countries holding a valid ordinary passport of their own country may enter, leave or transit the other country without a visa, provided that:

- each stay does not exceed 30 days
- the total stay does not exceed 90 days in any 180-day period

## When you still need a visa

The embassy notice also says you must apply for a visa before travel if you:

- plan to stay in China for more than 30 days
- are moving there to live
- plan to work, study, report as a journalist or do anything else that needs prior approval from the Chinese authorities

In those cases apply to the Chinese Embassy in Georgia.

The agreement covers ordinary passports only. Count your days: you cannot go over 30 days on one visit, and if you travel often, keep the 90-days-in-180 limit in mind.

## Hong Kong: separate rules

Hong Kong is a special administrative region of China with its own entry rules. The official table of the Hong Kong Immigration Department (updated August 2026) gives Georgian citizens a visa-free visit of 30 days.

The department's requirements for visitors:

- enough money to cover your stay
- a return or onward ticket (unless you are travelling on to mainland China or Macao)
- visitors may not work (paid or unpaid), set up or join a business, or study

If you want to stay in Hong Kong longer than 30 days, you must get a visa before you travel.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do Georgian citizens need a visa for China?**

No, as long as each visit is no longer than 30 days and you stay no more than 90 days in total in any 180 days. This applies to ordinary passports.

**Since when is travel to China visa-free?**

Since 00:00 Beijing time on 28 May 2024.

**When do I need a Chinese visa?**

If you stay longer than 30 days, or go to live, work, study, report as a journalist or do anything else that needs prior approval.

**Do Georgians need a visa for Hong Kong?**

No. According to the Hong Kong Immigration Department, Georgian citizens can visit visa-free for up to 30 days.

**Does your tour fit within the visa-free limits?**

Yes. On our 13-day tour we spend about 9 days in mainland China and three days in Hong Kong.
