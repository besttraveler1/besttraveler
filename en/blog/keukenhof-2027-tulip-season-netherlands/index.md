# Keukenhof 2027: When the Tulip Park in the Netherlands Is Open

Short answerAccording to the official site keukenhof.nl, Keukenhof is open from Thursday 18 March to Sunday 9 May 2027, daily from 8:00 to 19:00, with last entry at 18:15. Flowering depends on the weather, so nobody can say in advance exactly which week will be best.

## When is Keukenhof open in 2027?

The official Keukenhof website says the park is open from 18 March to 9 May 2027, every day including Sundays and public holidays, from 8:00 to 19:00. Entry closes at 18:15. The address is Stationsweg 166a, Lisse.

The flower season covers these spring weeks, so your travel dates need to fall inside that window.

## When do the tulips bloom?

Keukenhof says the flowering season follows the rhythm of nature and depends on the weather, which makes it hard to name the best time to visit. Early in the season you see early bloomers such as crocuses, daffodils, hyacinths and early tulips. Later, the later varieties come out, including larger tulips. The website also has a bloom report showing current conditions.

## Tickets

- According to the official FAQ, 2027 starting prices for adults are 21 EUR online and 27 EUR at the box office. Children aged 4-17 start at 11 EUR online, and under-4s go free.
- A ticket is valid only for the time slot you book, with no earlier or later entry.
- The site recommends buying online: it is cheaper and guarantees your preferred date and time.
- Visitors spend about 3 hours in the park on average.

If you are on one of our tours, you don't need to arrange a ticket yourself. Keukenhof is in the programme, and the entry price is shown on the tour page.

## How to plan

- Pick dates between 18 March and 9 May. Early and late in the season you see different flowers.
- According to the official site, the park is quieter before 10:30 and after 16:00. Morning and evening light is better for photos.
- Dress in layers and bring an umbrella or rain jacket, because spring weather changes fast.
- Wear comfortable shoes, since you will walk a lot.
