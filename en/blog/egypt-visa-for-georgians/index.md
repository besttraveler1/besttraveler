## Do Georgian citizens need a visa for Egypt?

Yes. The e-Visa portal of Egypt's Ministry of Interior (visa2egypt.gov.eg) says that a foreign citizen who wants to enter Egypt generally must first get an e-Visa. Georgia is on the portal's list of countries whose citizens can be issued one.

That means you do not need to visit an embassy. You fill in the form online, pay by card and receive the visa by email.

## How much does the Egypt visa cost in 2026?

As of October 2026, the official portal lists these tourist e-Visa fees:

- Single-entry tourist visa: 30 USD
- Multiple-entry tourist visa: 65 USD

You pay on the website with a credit or debit card. The portal's terms say the fee is non-refundable, so check every detail before you pay.

Our Egypt tours list a 'visa fee of 25 USD' as not included in the price. The official e-Visa portal currently shows 30 USD. Please confirm the exact amount and the type of visa with our manager when you book.

## How to get the e-Visa, step by step

- Go to the official portal visa2egypt.gov.eg and create a personal account.
- Fill in the application with your passport details. Parents can apply for their children from their own account.
- Pay the fee by card.
- Receive the e-Visa by email and print it. You must show the printout at the border.

The portal's home page says you should create your application at least 7 days before departure. Do not leave it to the last minute.

## Passport validity

Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months from your arrival date. The portal does not accept e-Visa applications with diplomatic, service, official and other special passports at this stage; holders of those passports should contact an Egyptian embassy.

## What the border officer may ask for

According to the portal, on arrival you may be asked for:

- a passport valid for at least 6 months from arrival
- the printed e-Visa
- your travel itinerary
- hotel bookings, or details of the places you will visit

The portal also makes clear that an e-Visa is not the final word on entry: the border authorities decide at the port of arrival.

## Visa on arrival and the 'Sinai only' stamp

Many travellers say you can also buy a visa at an Egyptian airport, and that there is a separate free stamp for Sharm el-Sheikh and the South Sinai resorts. We could not confirm either rule for Georgian citizens on the official Egyptian sources we were able to access.

Our advice: do not count on a visa at the airport. Get the e-Visa on the official portal before you fly, or confirm the rules with the Egyptian embassy. If you are going to Sharm el-Sheikh, ask the embassy about the Sinai stamp.

## Watch out for look-alike websites

Many agent websites look official and charge much more for the same visa. The official portal is visa2egypt.gov.eg. Do not enter your card details on any other site.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do Georgian citizens need a visa for Egypt?**

Yes. Georgian citizens can get an e-Visa on Egypt's official portal, visa2egypt.gov.eg.

**How much is the Egypt visa?**

As of October 2026 the official portal lists 30 USD for a single-entry tourist e-Visa and 65 USD for multiple entry. The fee is non-refundable.

**How long must my passport be valid?**

At least 6 months from the day you arrive in Egypt.

**How early should I apply?**

The portal says to create your application at least 7 days before departure.

**Can I get a visa at the airport?**

We could not confirm this for Georgian citizens on official sources. The safe option is to get the e-Visa in advance or check with the embassy.

**Does my child need a visa too?**

Yes, every traveller needs their own application. Parents can apply for their children from their own account.
