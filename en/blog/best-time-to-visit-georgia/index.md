# Best Time to Visit Georgia: Seasons and Months Explained

Published: 17 Oct 20262 min read

Photo: [ArcoVanDutch](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vineyard_in_Georgia_-_2018.jpg), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Short answerFor sightseeing and cities, the best times are late spring (May-June) and autumn (September-October). For the sea and the high mountains, choose July and August. The grape harvest (Rtveli) starts in Kakheti in September, and skiing usually runs from late December to April.

## In short: which season for what

- Sightseeing, cities, monasteries — May-June and September-October.
- Beach (Batumi, Adjara) — July and August.
- High mountains (Kazbegi, Juta, Svaneti) — summer and early autumn.
- Wine and the grape harvest — September-October in Kakheti.
- Skiing — late December to April.

## Spring (March-May)

In spring the lowlands turn green and the weather in the cities is pleasant. It is a good time for Tbilisi, Mtskheta, Kakheti and the semi-desert areas before the hot days start. In Batumi, spring is the driest time of year. High in the mountains there is still snow in March and April, and some roads and trails stay closed.

## Summer (June-August)

Summer is the season for the sea and the mountains. The warmest months in Batumi are July and August, with an average temperature of about 23 °C. Tbilisi is hot: the average July high is about 31 °C, so city walks are best in the morning or evening.

In the mountains this is the easiest time to walk: Kazbegi, Truso, Juta, Svaneti and Ushguli. Our Juta tour is a summer tour.

## Autumn (September-November)

For many people autumn is the best time in Georgia. The heat eases, the forests change colour and the grape harvest begins. According to Georgia's official tourism site, Rtveli starts in September with the Rkatsiteli harvest in Kakheti and runs until December, when Chkhaveri is picked in Guria and Adjara. A Kakheti wine tour is especially nice in September and October.

In Batumi, September to November are the wettest months of the year. If you go to the coast in autumn, have a plan for rainy days. The first snow can fall in the high mountains in late autumn.

## Winter (December-February)

Winter is mild in the lowlands: Tbilisi's average January high is about 6-7 °C. It is ski season: Gudauri, Bakuriani, Mestia and Goderdzi usually run from late December to April. Kazbegi is beautiful under snow, but the road sometimes closes because of avalanche risk. We cover winter in more detail in a separate article.

## For sightseeing tours

If your main goal is to see a lot of places, choose May-June or September-October. It is neither too hot nor too cold, and most mountain roads are open.

## Frequently asked questions

**When is the best time to visit Georgia?**

For sightseeing: May-June and September-October. For the sea and high mountains: July and August. For skiing: late December to April.

**When is beach season in Batumi?**

July and August are the warmest months. September to November bring the most rain in Batumi.

**When is Rtveli, the grape harvest?**

According to the official tourism site, it starts in Kakheti in September and continues until December in western Georgia.

**Is October a good time to visit Georgia?**

Yes, October is good for sightseeing and for Kakheti. On the coast it is already rainy.

**When should I go to the mountains in Georgia?**

In summer and early autumn, when roads and trails are open. In winter the mountains are for skiing.
