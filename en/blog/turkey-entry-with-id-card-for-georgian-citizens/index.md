# Turkey with a Georgian ID Card: Entry Rules for Georgian Citizens

## Can Georgian citizens enter Turkey with an ID card?

Yes. Türkiye's Ministry of Foreign Affairs publishes a list of 'countries whose citizens are allowed to enter Türkiye with their national IDs'. Georgia is on that list, together with Germany, France, Ukraine, Azerbaijan and others.

This means a Georgian citizen can cross at Sarpi, Vale or another land border, or fly to Istanbul, with a valid Georgian ID card. You can, of course, also travel on your passport.

Check that your ID card has not expired. Airlines may have their own rules on which documents they accept for boarding, so ask before you buy a ticket.

## How long can you stay in Turkey without a visa?

According to Türkiye's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, holders of Georgian ordinary and official passports are exempt from a visa for trips of up to 90 days. That is enough for a holiday, a family visit or a short trip.

The ministry's page does not explain how the 90 days are counted. If you travel to Turkey often or plan a long stay, check the rules with a Turkish embassy or consulate. If you are going to work, study or live in Turkey, ask the embassy which visa or permit you need.

## Travelling on a passport: how long must it be valid?

Under Turkish law, a foreigner's passport must be valid for at least 60 days beyond the end of the permitted stay. For example, for a 90-day stay the passport must have at least 150 days of validity on the day you enter.

Citizens of countries that may enter Türkiye with a national ID card are exempt from this 60-day rule, and Georgia is one of them. Even so, the ministry advises all travellers to have a document valid for at least 6 months from the date of arrival.

## What applies to children

The Turkish ministry's pages do not set out separate rules for children. The basic principle is simple: a child needs their own valid document, either a passport or an ID card. A child cannot travel on a parent's document.

When travelling with a child, check:

- that the child has their own document and it has not expired
- what consent or papers the child needs to leave Georgia if travelling with one parent, grandparents or a group
- the airline's rules for children travelling alone

Check the rules for a child leaving Georgia (for example with one parent or without parents) with the Public Service Hall or the border police before you travel.

## Quick checklist before you go

- A valid ID card or passport for every traveller, including children.
- A planned stay of under 90 days.
- For a child: their own document and, if needed, parental consent.
- If flying: the airline's document rules.
