## When to go to Cappadocia

GoTürkiye says balloon rides run all year, but the best time is spring or autumn, when the weather is generally milder and more regular than at other times. Its autumn feature names September and October as the best months to enjoy the landscape, when average temperatures drop to milder levels.

Cappadocia lies southeast of Ankara and is best known for its geology, above all the fairy chimneys. The Göreme Open-Air Museum has been on the UNESCO World Heritage List since 1985.

## How a balloon flight works

According to GoTürkiye, flights start just before sunrise. Guests start getting ready around 4:00 am, and a bus collects them from the hotel shortly after.

Everything depends on the weather. Türkiye's Directorate General of Civil Aviation (SHGM) has a separate circular on meteorological evaluation for balloon flights in Cappadocia. In practice, if it is windy or the weather is bad, flights can be delayed or cancelled that same morning.

A balloon flight is never guaranteed. If it doesn't happen, our programme also has time for photos with the balloons in the background. Ask about cancellation and refund terms when you book.

## What a flight costs on our tour

On our 6-day bus tour to Cappadocia, Amasya and Ordu, the balloon flight is optional at 200-250 EUR and is not included in the tour price. On the morning of day 3 you either fly or take photos of the balloons from the ground. The day continues with the Göreme Open-Air Museum, Tokali Church, Uchisar Castle and Pigeon Valley.

## What to wear

- Flights take off at dawn, and mornings are cool, especially in spring and autumn. Wear layers and bring a warm jacket.
- Comfortable closed shoes. Getting in and out of the basket can be awkward.
- A hat, against the sun and the heat from the burner.
- For walking in the valleys: trainers, water and sunglasses.
- For churches and monasteries: covered shoulders.

## Tips

- Book your flight early, with help from the tour escort.
- If you have a health condition, ask your doctor before flying.
- Don't take a cancellation personally: flights are cancelled for safety. The balloons look beautiful from the ground too.

## Frequently asked questions

**When is the best time for a balloon ride in Cappadocia?**

GoTürkiye says spring or autumn. For autumn it singles out September and October.

**Can the flight be cancelled?**

Yes. Flights depend on the weather and can be cancelled that same morning if it is windy or the weather is bad.

**How much is the balloon flight on your tour?**

It is optional and costs 200-250 EUR. It is not included in the tour price.

**What time does the flight start?**

Just before sunrise. Preparation starts at about 4:00 am.

**What should I wear?**

Warm layers, a jacket, closed shoes and a hat.
