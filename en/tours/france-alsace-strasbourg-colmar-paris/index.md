## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- Arrival at Beauvais airport
- Reims — a city in eastern France, founded in 80 BC
- Hotel check-in

- 

### Day 2

- Walk in Reims
- Porte de Mars — an ancient Roman triumphal arch, 3rd century AD
- Reims Cathedral (Notre-Dame de Reims) — where the kings of France were once crowned
- Metz — a city in north-eastern France
- Porte des Allemands
- Opéra-Théâtre de Metz — the oldest opera house in France and one of the oldest in Europe
- Drive to Saint-Hippolyte, hotel check-in

- 

### Day 3

- Strasbourg (free)
- Barrage Vauban, 17th century (free)
- 3 hours of free time to explore the city
- Scherwiller
- Riquewihr
- Return to the hotel

- 

### Day 4

- Eguisheim — named among the most beautiful villages of France in 2003; in 2006 it won a gold medal in the European contest 'Entente Florale'
- Ribeauvillé
- Kaysersberg
- Return to the hotel

- 

### Day 5

- Colmar — the third-largest town in Alsace
- Little Venice — a Colmar quarter of canals and half-timbered houses built by the water (optional boat ride)
- Drive to Paris, hotel check-in

- 

### Day 6

- Eiffel Tower
- Pont Alexandre III
- Musée d'Orsay
- Arc de Triomphe
- Champs-Élysées

- 

### Day 7

- Louvre
- Notre-Dame de Paris
- Sacré-Cœur
- Montmartre

- 

### Day 8

- Versailles
- Flight from Beauvais airport
