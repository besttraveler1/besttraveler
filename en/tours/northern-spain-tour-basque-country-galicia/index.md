## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- Flight from Kutaisi at 12:20
- Arrival in Madrid at 15:30
- Transfer to the hotel

- 

### Day 2

- Aranda de Duero — a historic town in the Duero valley
- Bodega Histórica Don Carlos — a historic underground wine cellar under the old town (tour + 2 wine tastings, 15 EUR)
- San Esteban de Gormaz — a historic town in the province of Soria
- Calatañazor — one of the most beautiful medieval villages in Spain
- Soria

- 

### Day 3

- Monastery of San Juan de Duero — 12th–13th century
- Hermitage of San Saturio — an 18th-century chapel built into the rock
- Church of Santo Domingo — a 12th-century Romanesque church
- Co-cathedral of San Pedro — the main church of Soria, 12th century
- Vitoria-Gasteiz — capital of the Basque Country

- 

### Day 4

- Lantern Museum (Museo de los Faroles) — 267 coloured glass lanterns from the Virgen Blanca lantern procession
- Old Cathedral of Santa María — building began in the 13th century
- Bilbao — a Basque city of modern architecture, medieval streets and famous food
- Santander

- 

### Day 5

- Magdalena Palace — built 1909–1911, summer residence of King Alfonso XIII and Queen Victoria Eugenie
- El Capricho — one of Antoni Gaudí's early works, 1883–1885 (7 EUR)
- Oviedo — founded in the 8th century, historic capital of the Kingdom of Asturias

- 

### Day 6

- Lugo
- A Coruña — a Galician city on the Atlantic coast
- Crystal Beach (Praia dos Cristais) — a small cove covered with sea-polished pieces of coloured glass
- Sanctuary of the Virgin of the Boat (Virxe da Barca) — a well-known Galician pilgrimage site (11th–12th century)

- 

### Day 7

- Santiago de Compostela — capital of Galicia and one of Spain's most important historic and religious cities
- Templar Castle of Ponferrada — given to the Knights Templar by the kings of León in 1178 (6 EUR)
- Valladolid — seat of the Spanish royal court in the 16th century

- 

### Day 8

- Alcázar of Segovia — royal residence of the kings of Castile from the 12th century (10 EUR)
- Transfer to the airport
- Flight to Kutaisi at 16:40
