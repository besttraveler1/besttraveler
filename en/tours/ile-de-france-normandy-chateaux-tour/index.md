## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- Flight from Kutaisi at 15:55
- Arrival at Beauvais at 18:10
- Transfer to the hotel

- 

### Day 2

- Château de Champs-sur-Marne, 18th century (9 EUR)
- La Chocolaterie Menier, 19th century
- Château de Grosbois, 17th century (20 EUR)
- Château de Vaux-le-Vicomte, 17th century (18 EUR)

- 

### Day 3

- Château de Maintenon (13 EUR)
- Château de Villebon (10 EUR)
- La Chapelle-Montligeon — the Basilica of Notre-Dame de Montligeon
- Alençon

- 

### Day 4

- Château de Carrouges, Normandy (7 EUR)
- Château de Sassy, 18th century (8 EUR)
- Lisieux — the Basilica of Saint Thérèse

- 

### Day 5

- Deauville — a well-known Normandy seaside resort
- Honfleur — a historic port town
- Victor Hugo Museum, Villequier (6 EUR)
- Lyons-la-Forêt — one of the prettiest villages in Normandy

- 

### Day 6

- Château de Bizy (10 EUR)
- Château d'Auvers — linked with Vincent van Gogh (Van Gogh exhibition 12 EUR)
- Château de Maisons-Laffitte, 17th century (9 EUR)

- 

### Day 7

- Château de Compiègne — a royal and imperial residence (10 EUR)
- Donjon de Septmonts
- Val d'Igny Abbey and the monastery chocolate workshop
- Épernay — the Museum of Champagne and Regional Archaeology (10 EUR)
- Amiens

- 

### Day 8

- Notre-Dame d'Amiens Cathedral (towers 7 EUR, treasury 5 EUR)
- Transfer to the airport
- Flight to Kutaisi at 18:45
