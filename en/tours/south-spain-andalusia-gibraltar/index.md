## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- Flight from Tbilisi at 04:40 (Turkish Airlines)
- Arrival in Málaga at 11:15
- Castillo de Colomares — a monument to Christopher Columbus and the discovery of America (3.50 EUR)
- Marbella — one of the best-known towns of the Costa del Sol
- Salvador Dalí sculptures

- 

### Day 2

- Gibraltar — a British Overseas Territory on the southern coast of Spain
- Europa Point Lighthouse — at the southern tip of Gibraltar; opened in 1841 and still working. Views over the Strait of Gibraltar and, in good weather, the coast of North Africa
- Algeciras
- Tarifa — the southernmost point of Andalusia, where the Atlantic Ocean meets the Mediterranean Sea

- 

### Day 3

- Baelo Claudia — ruins of an ancient Roman town (2nd century BC)
- Cádiz — one of the oldest seaside towns of Andalusia, on the Atlantic coast
- Arcos de la Frontera — an Andalusian 'white town'

- 

### Day 4

- Palace of Ribera, Bornos
- Ronda — one of the most striking towns in the province of Málaga
- Setenil de las Bodegas — a 'white village' in the province of Cádiz, with houses built right under and into the rocks

- 

### Day 5

- Olvera — one of the 'white villages' (Pueblos Blancos) of the province of Cádiz
- Seville — the capital of Andalusia

- 

### Day 6

- Córdoba — one of the most important historic cities of Andalusia
- Priego de Córdoba — a small town in Andalusia
- Granada

- 

### Day 7

- Alhambra (25 EUR)
- Antequera — a historic town in the province of Málaga
- Mijas Pueblo

- 

### Day 8

- Málaga
- Transfer to the airport
- Flight to Tbilisi at 17:40
- Arrival in Tbilisi the next morning at 05:40
