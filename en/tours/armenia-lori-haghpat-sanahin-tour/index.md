## Tour programme

- 
- Akhtala, 10th-century Georgian monastery
- Haghpat Monastery, 10th–13th centuries (UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1996)
- Sanahin Monastery, 10th–13th centuries (UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1996)
- Odzun, 6th-century church
- Kobayr, Georgian monastery known for its unique frescoes
- Tamar's Bridge in the town of Alaverdi
