- 

### Day 1

- Flight from Tbilisi
- Arrival at Düsseldorf airport

- Kleve, Germany

### Day 2

- Kasteel Doornenburg, a well-preserved medieval castle (13 EUR)
- Middachten Castle, with a history going back to 1190 (garden 8 EUR, castle and garden 15 EUR)
- Apeldoorn
- Paleis Het Loo, a 17th-century royal palace (21 EUR)

- 

### Day 3

- Zwolle, a historic Hanseatic town
- Giethoorn, the 'Venice of the Netherlands', a small village with canals, wooden bridges and thatched houses
- Alkmaar, one of the most beautiful historic towns in North Holland

- 

### Day 4

- Zaandam
- Zaanse Schans: windmills, green wooden houses, cheese makers and clog-making demonstrations
- Haarlem, a beautiful historic town

- 

### Day 5

- Amsterdam

- 

### Day 6

- Keukenhof, a world-famous spring flower park (25 EUR)
- De Haar Castle, the largest castle in the Netherlands
- Utrecht, one of the oldest towns in the Netherlands

- 

### Day 7

- Doorwerth Castle, a 13th-century medieval castle
- Nijmegen, considered one of the oldest towns in the Netherlands
- Düsseldorf (Germany)

- 

### Day 8

- Transfer to the airport
