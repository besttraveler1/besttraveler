Tuscany, Elba and San Marino — 10-Day Tour of Italy

The tour starts in Rome and travels by car through towns of Lazio, Tuscany and Umbria, with Elba island and the Republic of San Marino. Each night is spent in a different town.

Tour programme

  1. Day 1

    • Arrival in Rome
    • Anguillara Sabazia
    • Hotel check-in (Anguillara Sabazia)

  2. Day 2

    • Bracciano
    • Orsini-Odescalchi Castle (10 EUR)
    • Tolfa
    • Bagnaia
    • Overnight in Bagnaia

  3. Day 3

    • Bagnaia
    • Villa Lante
    • Fountain of the Deluge
    • St Patrick's Well
    • Terme di Saturnia
    • Overnight at Terme di Saturnia

  4. Day 4

    • Hot springs and travertine pools — 2 hours to bathe
    • Piombino
    • Elba island
    • Portoferraio (Elba)
    • San Martino (Elba)
    • Napoleon's residence at Villa San Martino (5 EUR)
    • Piombino
    • Overnight in Piombino

  5. Day 5

    • Pisa
    • Villa Torrigiani
    • San Gimignano
    • Overnight in San Gimignano

  6. Day 6

    • Torre Grossa (10 EUR)
    • Campatelli tower house
    • Siena
    • Siena Cathedral
    • Porta Romana (Siena)
    • Porta Camollia (Siena)
    • Overnight in Siena

  7. Day 7

    • Sinalunga
    • Arezzo
    • Arezzo Cathedral
    • Republic of San Marino, one of the world's smallest republics
    • Overnight in San Marino

  8. Day 8

    • San Marino
    • Montale tower
    • Guaita fortress (10 EUR)
    • Basilica of San Marino
    • Perugia
    • Overnight in Perugia

  9. Day 9

    • Perugina Chocolate House (10 EUR)
    • La Scarzuola (10 EUR)
    • Marmore Falls, upper Belvedere (13 EUR)
    • Fiumicino
    • Overnight in Fiumicino

  10. Day 10

    • Transfer to the airport
    • Flight to Kutaisi at 08:20
