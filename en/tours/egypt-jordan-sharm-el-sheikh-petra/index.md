## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- Departure from Tbilisi International Airport
- Arrival in Sharm el-Sheikh, transfer to the hotel, free time
- City tour: the bazaar, the Old Market, Naama Bay and Farsha Café

- 

### Day 2

- Optional yacht tour (60 USD): departure at 08:00, four stops, diving and snorkelling, a visit to White Island, meals, cold and hot drinks, return at 16:30

- 

### Day 3

- Petra, Jordan — a historic and archaeological city nicknamed the "Rose City" for the colour of its stone
- Departure from the hotel at 01:00 by air-conditioned Mercedes bus to Nuweiba port, then about 1 hour by ferry to Aqaba and on to Petra
- The visit starts on foot through a narrow, winding canyon more than 1 km long
- Al-Khazneh (the Treasury) — at the end of the canyon, through a very narrow passage, one of the most impressive sights of Petra
- Roman theatre and the Royal Tombs
- Lunch at a restaurant in the town centre
- Return to Aqaba at 18:45, free time for shopping
- Ferry back to Nuweiba and bus to Sharm el-Sheikh

- 

### Day 4

- Optional big safari (40 USD): quad bikes into the desert, tea in a Bedouin village, camel ride, dinner and a show with traditional dances and a snake show

- 

### Day 5

- Optional Cairo trip (85 USD): the National Museum, the pyramids, the Sphinx, how essences, perfumes and papyrus are made, dinner on the Nile

- 

### Day 6

- Optional (100 USD): jeep tour to the Red Canyon, Dahab, Mount Sinai and St Catherine's Monastery — the place where, by tradition, Moses received the Ten Commandments, and the Burning Bush
- Or optional (50 USD): jeep tour to the Red and White Canyons, quad tour and camel ride

- 

### Day 7

- Optional glass-bottom boat trip on the Red Sea to see sea life (30 USD)

- 

### Day 8

- Transfer to the airport and flight back to Tbilisi
