Tour programme

  1. Day 1

    • Flight from Tbilisi to Sri Lanka

  2. Day 2

    • Meeting at the airport and drive to Sigiriya
    • Sigiriya Village Tour
    • Dambulla Cave Temple
    • Elephant riding and feeding
    • Overnight at a hotel in Sigiriya

  3. Day 3: Sigiriya to Kandy

    • Sigiriya Lion Rock
    • Herbal and spice garden
    • Hindu temple
    • Kandy cultural dance show and museum
    • Overnight at a hotel in Kandy

  4. Day 4: Kandy to Nuwara Eliya

    • Temple of the Tooth
    • Royal Botanical Garden
    • Tea factory and tea plantation
    • Ramboda Waterfall
    • Overnight at a hotel in Nuwara Eliya

  5. Day 5: Nuwara Eliya to Ella

    • Scenic train journey (2-3 hours)
    • Nine Arches Bridge
    • Little Adam's Peak
    • Overnight at a hotel in Ella

  6. Day 6: Ella to Yala and Mirissa

    • Ravana Waterfall
    • Yala National Park, evening safari
    • Mirissa Beach
    • Overnight at a hotel in Mirissa

  7. Day 7: Mirissa to Galle and Colombo

    • Whale watching tour
    • Galle Fort
    • Turtle hatchery and conservation centre
    • Coconut Tree Hill viewpoint
    • Overnight at a hotel in Colombo

  8. Day 8: Colombo to the Maldives

    • Gangaramaya Temple
    • Independence Square
    • Galle Face Green, Colombo's seaside promenade
    • Evening transfer to the airport, flight to Male (Maldives)
    • Transfer to the hotel

  9. Days 9-10

    • Free time in the Maldives

  10. Day 11

    • Transfer to the airport
    • Flight to Tbilisi
