## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- Arrival at Beauvais airport
- Gerberoy — a medieval village known as the 'village of a thousand roses'

- 

### Day 2

- Dieppe — a Normandy seaside town on the English Channel
- Veules-les-Roses — a member of the Most Beautiful Villages of France association
- Étretat — white chalk cliffs and natural stone arches
- Rouen — the historic capital of Normandy

- 

### Day 3

- Rouen: the Gothic Notre-Dame Cathedral, the Gros-Horloge astronomical clock and the Old Market Square
- Domaine du Champ de Bataille, 17th century (gardens 15 EUR, château and gardens 27 EUR)
- Fougères — a historic town in Brittany
- Château de Fougères, with 13 towers (10 EUR)

- 

### Day 4

- Mont-Saint-Michel — a village and abbey on a rocky island, a UNESCO site
- Mont-Saint-Michel Abbey (20 EUR)
- Saint-Malo — a Brittany seaside town with a walled old town
- Dinan — a medieval town in the Rance valley
- Château de Goulaine near Nantes

- 

### Day 5

- La Rochelle — an Atlantic port town with an old harbour
- Fort Boyard — a sea fort in the Atlantic near La Rochelle
- Chinon — a Loire Valley town with a royal fortress

- 

### Day 6

- Château d’Ussé — white towers and terraced gardens (15 EUR)
- Amboise — a historic town on the Loire
- Château de Chenonceau — the château built like a bridge over the river Cher (18 EUR)

- 

### Day 7

- Château de Chambord — the largest Renaissance château of the Loire Valley (17 EUR)
- Château de Fontainebleau — a royal palace, a UNESCO site (14 EUR)

- 

### Day 8

- Château de Chantilly — a château on the water with gardens and stables (18 EUR)
