Nile Cruise: Luxor, Aswan, Abu Simbel, Hurghada and El Gouna

An 8-day tour of Egypt with a Georgian-speaking tour leader from Tbilisi. It includes St Anthony's Monastery, 4 nights on a 5-star Nile cruise ship, the temples of Luxor and Aswan, and El Gouna.

Tour programme

  1. Day 1

    • Flight from Tbilisi airport at 04:25
    • Arrival in Hurghada at 09:30
    • St Anthony's Monastery — the world's oldest working Christian monastery. St Anthony the Great (251–356) lived as a hermit and died in a cave in the cliff above it
    • Church of St Anthony with very old frescoes
    • Church of St Paul of Thebes
    • Old library and manuscripts
    • Monks' cells

  2. Day 2

    • Luxor
    • Karnak Temple — one of the grandest monuments of ancient Egypt (20th century BC – 1st century AD)
    • Temple of Hatshepsut — a unique monument of ancient Egyptian architecture (15th century BC)
    • Valley of the Kings — one of the most important necropolises of ancient Egypt (16th–11th centuries BC)

  3. Day 3

    • Hot-air balloon flight (optional, extra cost)
    • Luxor Temple — one of the most elegant and historically important temples of ancient Egypt (14th century BC)
    • Free time on the ship

  4. Day 4

    • Temple of Edfu — one of the best-preserved temples of ancient Egypt, dedicated to the god Horus (237–57 BC)
    • Temple of Kom Ombo — unique because it is dedicated to two gods at once (2nd–1st centuries BC)

  5. Day 5

    • Abu Simbel (optional, 200 USD) — two temples cut into the rock by Ramesses II in the 13th century BC: the Great Temple (Ramesses II) and the Small Temple (Nefertari)
    • Philae Temple — one of the best-known temples in Egypt, dedicated to the goddess Isis
    • Nubian village (optional, 50 USD) — a small traditional village on the Nile, known for its colourful houses, art and hospitality

  6. Day 6

    • Optional (250 USD): Wadi es-Sebua — an ancient Egyptian temple complex, mostly from the New Kingdom (13th century BC)
    • Optional: Temple of Derr — a small but historically important temple built under Ramesses II (13th century BC)
    • Travel to Hurghada

  7. Day 7

    • El Gouna — a modern resort on the Red Sea coast

  8. Day 8

    • Flight to Tbilisi at 03:20
    • Arrival at 15:50
