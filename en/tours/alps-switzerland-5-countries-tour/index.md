## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- Flight from Tbilisi at 13:45
- Arrival at Geneva airport at 16:30
- Geneva

- 

### Day 2

- Annecy (France) — a town often called 'the Venice of the Alps'
- Yvoire — a medieval village on the French shore of Lake Geneva
- Château de Chillon
- Hotel (Switzerland)

- 

### Day 3

- Gruyères — a medieval Swiss town known for its Gruyère cheese, castle and Alpine views
- Château de Gruyères (15 EUR)
- Jaun Waterfall (Wasserfall von Jaun)
- Lauterbrunnen — one of Switzerland's most beautiful valleys, the 'valley of 72 waterfalls'
- Gimmelwald — an Alpine village above the Lauterbrunnen valley, at about 1,367 m (by cable car)
- Mürren — an Alpine village above the valley, at about 1,638 m (Gimmelwald–Mürren cable car 35 EUR)
- Grindelwald — one of Switzerland's best-known Alpine resorts
- Hotel (Switzerland)

- 

### Day 4

- Lucerne (Switzerland) — known for its covered wooden bridge built in 1333, with 17th-century paintings inside
- Einsiedeln Abbey — a Benedictine abbey dating back to the 10th century
- Balzers — a small town in Liechtenstein
- Vaduz — the capital of the Principality of Liechtenstein
- Lech am Arlberg — a well-known Alpine resort in Austria
- Hotel (Austria)

- 

### Day 5

- Linderhof Palace (Germany) — the only one of King Ludwig II of Bavaria's three palaces that he saw completed
- Ettal Abbey (Kloster Ettal) — a Benedictine monastery in the Bavarian Alps
- Oberammergau — a Bavarian village known for its painted houses, woodcarving and the Passion Play
- Hotel (Germany)

- 

### Day 6

- Neuschwanstein (Germany) — one of the most famous castles in the world, built for Ludwig II from 1869
- Meersburg — a historic town on Lake Constance (Bodensee)
- Rhine Falls (Rheinfall), Switzerland
- Baden (Switzerland) — a small historic spa town
- Basel — a city in north-western Switzerland
- Thun (Switzerland) — a town on Lake Thun and the Aare river, known for its medieval castle and old town
- Hotel (Switzerland)

- 

### Day 7

- Bern — the Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site
- Palais de Rumine, Lausanne
- Geneva

- 

### Day 8

- Transfer to the airport
- Flight to Tbilisi at 06:30
