## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- 07:00 departure from Tbilisi to the Kartsakhi border crossing, border formalities
- Kajis Tsikhe, a 10th-century fortress
- Kars Castle (1153)
- Kars, hotel check-in, free time

- 

### Day 2

- Ani Cathedral (built 989-1001)
- Ani archaeological site
- Ishak Pasha Palace (1784)
- Muradiye Waterfall
- Van, hotel check-in

- 

### Day 3

- Van Fortress (9th-7th century BC)
- Hosap Castle (17th century)
- Akdamar Island (boat about 15-30 Turkish lira)
- Mardin, hotel check-in

- 

### Day 4

- Deyrulzafaran Monastery, founded in the 5th century (20-30 Turkish lira)
- Mor Gabriel Monastery, founded in 397, one of the oldest Christian monasteries in the world
- Diyarbakir
- Mardin: walk and free time

- 

### Day 5

- Harran, one of the oldest cities in the world
- Sanliurfa, one of the oldest cities
- The sunken town of Halfeti, boat trip
- Adiyaman, capital of Adiyaman province, with a rich history
- Hotel check-in

- 

### Day 6

- Perre (Pirin), an ancient city in Adiyaman province (4th-3rd century BC)
- Mount Nemrut (69-34 BC), a UNESCO World Heritage site with large statues and royal reliefs made on the orders of King Antiochus I of Commagene
- Bingol

- 

### Day 7

- Return to Tbilisi
