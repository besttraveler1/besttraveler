Yerevan in 3 Days: Sevan, Garni, Geghard, Etchmiadzin

A different three-day tour to Yerevan, via Dilijan and Lake Sevan. On the second and third days we see Yerevan and its surroundings.

Tour programme

  1. Day 1

    • Departure from Tbilisi at 06:00, border procedures
    • Stop at a picnic spot near Alaverdi
    • Dilijan (free)
    • Stop at a food place by Lake Sevan
    • Sevanavank Monastery (free)
    • Yerevan, Republic Square (free), free time

  2. Day 2

    • Yerevan, the Cascade (free)
    • Matenadaran (15 GEL)
    • Sergei Parajanov House-Museum (15 GEL)
    • Khor Virap (free)
    • Eco-village by Lake Azat (free)
    • Garni (15 GEL)
    • Geghard Monastery (free)
    • Charents Arch (free)

  3. Day 3

    • Ararat Museum (35 GEL)
    • Zvartnots (15 GEL)
    • St Hripsime Church (free)
    • Etchmiadzin Monastery (free)
    • Return to Tbilisi
