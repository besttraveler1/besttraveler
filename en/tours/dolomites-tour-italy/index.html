Northern Italy and the Dolomites: Treviso, Bolzano, Lake Braies — 6–13 July

An 8-day group tour of northern Italy by car. Flight from Kutaisi to Venice, Venetian villas and Treviso, then the mountains, lakes and Alpine villages of the Dolomites.

Tour programme

  1. Day 1

    • Flight from Kutaisi at 22:50
    • Arrival in Venice at 01:00
    • Transfer to the hotel

  2. Day 2

    • Villa Widmann Rezzonico Foscari — built in the early 18th century for the Seriman family (7 EUR)
    • Roncade — a 16th-century Renaissance castle and Venetian villa (15 EUR)
    • Treviso — 'little Venice'

  3. Day 3

    • Villa di Maser — a famous work of the architect Andrea Palladio, a UNESCO World Heritage site (12 EUR)
    • San Martino di Castrozza — an Alpine resort at about 1,450 m in the heart of the Dolomites, below the Pale di San Martino
    • Cable car to the Altopiano delle Pale — a rocky high plateau at about 2,500–2,700 m (2 cable cars, Colverde + Rosetta, return 34 EUR)

  4. Day 4

    • Baita Segantini — an Alpine lake in the Dolomites (walk about 3 km one way, or the Passo Rolle–Baita Segantini shuttle bus, 10 EUR)
    • Moena — an Alpine village at about 1,150–1,200 m, called the 'fairy-tale town of the Dolomites'

  5. Day 5

    • Lake Carezza, the 'rainbow lake' — an Alpine lake at about 1,520 m
    • Castel Roncolo — a medieval castle built in 1237 (10 EUR)
    • Bolzano
    • Renon cable car and the narrow-gauge railway (running since 1907) on the Renon plateau at about 1,200 m: Alpine villages and the earth pyramids — tall clay pillars (cable car + train, 20 EUR)

  6. Day 6

    • Ortisei — an Alpine town in South Tyrol, in the heart of the Dolomites
    • Seceda (optional) — cable car in two stages up to about 2,500 m, with views of the jagged Dolomite ridge (return ticket 74 EUR)
    • Lake Braies — one of the best-known lakes in the Dolomites, at about 1,496 m; optional ride in a wooden boat
    • Prato alla Drava — a small, quiet Alpine village

  7. Day 7

    • San Vito di Cadore — an Alpine village, and Lake Mosigo
    • Belluno — the 'gateway to the Dolomites'
    • Portogruaro — a town founded in 1140

  8. Day 8

    • Caorle Lagoon — traditional fishermen's huts, boat trip (24 EUR, 2.5 hours)
    • Transfer to the airport
    • Flight to Kutaisi at 16:35
