### Programme

- 05:00 departure from Tbilisi, border crossing at Vale
- Tbeti monastery complex, religious centre of a powerful diocese and a Georgian centre of writing
- Satle fortress, centre of the Shavsheti duchy
- Hell's Canyon, a natural wonder of Klarjeti
- Artanuji, fortress town and citadel, a capital of the Georgian kingdom, with its strong citadel and remains of town life
- Landscapes of Klarjeti
- Doliskana Monastery
- Yusufeli
- Oshki monastery complex and cathedral
- Tortum gorge and Tortum Waterfall
- Otkhta Eklesia monastery complex
- Parkhali Monastery, from the time of David Kuropalates and the Tao-Klarjeti renaissance
- Chapels of Parkhali
- Former summer residences of the Bagrationi
- Ruins of Bana cathedral and the old town
- Shopping and a walk in Ardahan (Artaani)
- Return to Georgia
