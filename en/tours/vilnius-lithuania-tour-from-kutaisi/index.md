## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- Flight from Kutaisi at 06:30
- Arrival in Vilnius at 09:35
- Transfer to the hotel
- Vilnius Old Town — a UNESCO World Heritage Site
- Vilnius Cathedral
- Gediminas Tower

### Day 2

- Palace of the Grand Dukes of Lithuania — an important history museum
- Gate of Dawn — the only surviving gate of the old town walls, with a famous icon of the Mother of God
- St. Anne's Church — one of the most beautiful Gothic buildings in Vilnius
- Church of St. Francis of Assisi (Bernardine Church)

### Day 3

- Church of Sts. Peter and Paul
- Vilnius University — founded in 1579, one of the oldest universities in Central and Eastern Europe
- Town Hall Square

- 

### Day 4

- Town of Trakai
- Trakai Island Castle, 14th century

- 

### Day 5

- Transfer to the airport
- Flight to Kutaisi at 08:45
