# Yerevan in 2 Days: Sevan, Etchmiadzin, Garni and Geghard

A standard two-day minibus tour to Yerevan. On day one we stop in Dilijan, at Lake Sevan and in Etchmiadzin; on day two we see Yerevan, Garni and Geghard.
