# Sicily and Southern Italy — 8-Day Tour from Yerevan

The tour starts with a flight from Yerevan to Italy, follows the coast of southern Italy and crosses to Sicily by ferry. On the island we visit Palermo, Monreale, Agrigento, the baroque towns and Catania.

## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- Flight from Yerevan at 12:05
- Arrival in Naples at 14:15
- Salerno, one of the most beautiful towns in southern Italy

- 

### Day 2

- Paola
- Sanctuary of St Francis of Paola, an important pilgrimage site in southern Italy where St Francis of Paola rests
- Chiesetta di Piedigrotta, a church carved entirely into a seaside rock, with dozens of statues also carved from the rock
- Villa San Giovanni
- Ferry crossing to Sicily
- Messina, Sicily's third-largest city and the main port gateway from mainland Italy

- 

### Day 3

- Cefalù, one of the most beautiful seaside towns in Sicily
- Palermo, the capital and largest city of Sicily

- 

### Day 4

- Monreale, a historic hillside town known for its Norman cathedral, built in 1174–1189 by order of King William II; a UNESCO World Heritage site
- Marinella di Selinunte
- Selinunte Archaeological Park, the largest archaeological park in Europe
- Agrigento, founded by the Greeks in the 6th century BC

- 

### Day 5

- Villa Romana del Casale, one of the best-preserved Roman villas
- Caltagirone, a historic town in central Sicily known for its ceramics
- Ragusa, one of the most beautiful baroque towns in Sicily
- Modica, a UNESCO site (Late Baroque towns of the Val di Noto), known as the capital of chocolate

- 

### Day 6

- Marzamemi, a historic fishing village
- Noto
- Aci Castello castle
- Aci Trezza
- Catania, Sicily's second-largest city

- 

### Day 7

- Catania
- Transfer to the airport
- Flight to Tbilisi at 17:40

- 

### Day 8

- Arrival in Tbilisi at 01:35
