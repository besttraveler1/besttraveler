## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- Flight from Tbilisi at 03:25
- Arrival in Hurghada at 10:15
- Transfer to the hotel
- Free time

- 

### Day 2

- Excursion "Port Said and the Suez Canal": early departure from Hurghada with a Russian-speaking guide
- Al-Salam Mosque — one of the most beautiful mosques of Port Fuad, on the bank of the Suez Canal
- St Bishoy Coptic Church — one of Egypt's best-known Coptic churches, where you learn about the history and traditions of Egyptian Christianity
- Free ferry crossing of the Suez Canal, with a close look at large ocean ships
- Salt Mountain in Port Fuad — a snow-white hill of sea salt, a popular photo spot
- Lunch at a restaurant with a view of the Mediterranean Sea
- Free time in the Port Said pedestrian zone for a walk and souvenirs
- Port Said Lighthouse at the entrance to the Suez Canal
- Site of the Ferdinand de Lesseps statue — the story of building the Suez Canal
- Monument on Egypt Square, dedicated to the history of Port Said
- Photo stop at the decorative gate — one of the symbols of modern Port Said
- Return to Hurghada

- 

### Day 3

- Free day
- Optional safari (25 USD): jeeps, quad bikes, buggies, a Bedouin village, camels

- 

### Day 4

- Hula Hula island: by yacht or by a large boat with glass windows (your choice)
- 3–3.5 hours on the island with swimming
- Lunch on the island
- 5-minute massage
- Volleyball
- Banana boat ride

- 

### Day 5

- Luxor tour by tourist minibus with a guide
- Karnak Temple
- Colossi of Memnon
- Crossing the Nile
- Temple of Queen Hatshepsut
- Valley of the Kings
- Lunch
- Return to Hurghada

- 

### Day 6

- Monasteries of St Anthony and St Paul

- 

### Day 7

- Free day
- Transfer to the airport at 20:00
- Flight to Tbilisi
