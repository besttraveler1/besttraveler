Turkey and Greece — 14-Day Tour by Bus

A 14-day bus tour along Turkey's Aegean and Mediterranean coast, with two days on the Greek islands of Chios and Rhodes. 4–5 star hotels with breakfast and dinner.

Tour programme

  1. Day 1

    • 05:00 departure from Tbilisi towards Sarpi, border procedures
    • Tokat
    • Tokat castle (10–20 TRY)
    • Check in at the hotel

  2. Day 2

    • Ankara — the capital of Turkey
    • Anıtkabir — Atatürk's mausoleum (30–50 TRY)
    • Ankara citadel
    • Eskişehir, check in at the hotel

  3. Day 3

    • Sazova Park in Eskişehir: Shakespeare's House and a small zoo
    • Boat ride on the Porsuk river (10–20 TRY)
    • Walk through the city streets
    • Night at the hotel

  4. Day 4

    • Bergama — ancient Pergamon
    • Acropolis of Pergamon (100–150 TRY)
    • Pergamon theatre and Temple of Zeus
    • Asclepion
    • Izmir — check in at the hotel, evening walk

  5. Day 5

    • The old port of Smyrna
    • Agora — the ancient Greek market
    • Kadifekale
    • Kemeraltı bazaar
    • Alaçatı — free time
    • Çeşme, check in at the hotel

  6. Day 6

    • Ferry to the Greek island of Chios (50–60 EUR)
    • A full day on the island: sights and time at the sea
    • Night at the hotel in Çeşme

  7. Day 7

    • Çeşme castle (20–30 TRY)
    • Ephesus (150–200 TRY)
    • House of the Virgin Mary near Ephesus
    • Şirince village
    • Akyaka, check in at the hotel

  8. Day 8

    • Boat ride on the river
    • Free time at the sea or in the village
    • 14:00 drive to Marmaris
    • Marmaris castle (50–75 TRY)
    • Check in at the hotel, walk along the seafront

  9. Day 9

    • The Greek island of Rhodes (50–60 EUR)
    • Rhodes old town — a UNESCO World Heritage site
    • Rhodes fortress and the Palace of the Grand Master
    • Free time at the sea
    • Night at the hotel in Marmaris

  10. Day 10

    • Ancient city of Kaunos (excursion 150–250 TRY)
    • Kaunos rock tombs (50–100 TRY)
    • Göcek — check in at the hotel, free time

  11. Day 11

    • Side
    • Roman theatre (50–60 TRY)
    • Temple of Apollo (50–60 TRY)
    • Ancient forum and market
    • Check in at the hotel, free time

  12. Day 12

    • Göreme
    • Check in at the hotel, free time

  13. Day 13

    • Giresun
    • Check in at the hotel, free time

  14. Day 14

    • Return to Tbilisi (a stop at a shopping centre if the group wishes)

Included in the price

  • Transport by bus or minibus, with internet across Turkey
  • Tour organisation and a short overview of each sight
  • Photos
  • 4–5 star hotels with breakfast and dinner
  • Nights: Tokat, Eskişehir (2), Izmir, Çeşme (2), Akyaka, Marmaris (2), Göcek, Side, Göreme, Giresun

Not included

  • Ferry to the Greek islands (120 EUR)
  • Museum tickets (1200–1500 TRY)

Good to know

  • Meeting point: Didube metro station, upper exit
  • Documents: International passport valid for at least 6 months
  • An ID card is enough for Turkey, but the Greek islands (Chios, Rhodes) need an international passport.
  • Meeting point: next to Didube metro station (upper metro exit).
  • No alcohol or smoking in the vehicle.
