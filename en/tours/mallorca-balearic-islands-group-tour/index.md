## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- Flight from Kutaisi at 14:35
- Arrival in Barcelona at 17:40
- Flight to Palma de Mallorca at 20:50
- Arrival in Palma at 21:45
- Transfer to the hotel

- 

### Day 2

- Palma de Mallorca — capital of the Balearic Islands
- Palma Cathedral (La Seu) — a 14th–16th century Gothic cathedral, the symbol of the city (11 EUR)
- Royal Palace of La Almudaina — first an Arab fortress, now one of the Spanish royal family's residences (8 EUR)
- Bellver Castle — a round 14th-century Gothic castle (4 EUR)
- Old town

- 

### Day 3

- Free time to swim at Playa de Illetes — white sand, clear turquoise water and small rocky islets (2–3 hours)
- Valldemossa — one of Mallorca's most beautiful villages
- Gardens of Alfàbia — a historic garden
- Cala de Deià — a small cove with turquoise water

- 

### Day 4

- Deià — a village in the Serra de Tramuntana mountains
- Sóller — a historic town of Mallorca
- Sóller train (Tren de Sóller) — a historic wooden train linking Palma and Sóller since 1912. The ride takes about 1 hour through mountains, tunnels and orange groves (one way 23 EUR, return 30 EUR)
- Port de Sóller

- 

### Day 5

- Optional boat trip — 4 options to choose from (see extras)
- Sanctuary of Lluc — a pilgrimage site founded in the 13th century, home of the statue of the Virgin, patron of Mallorca

- 

### Day 6

- Alcúdia — a historic town
- Cap de Formentor
- Caló des Moro — a natural cove, free time to swim

- 

### Day 7

- Palma de Mallorca
- Flight to Barcelona at 16:35
- Arrival in Barcelona at 17:30

- 

### Day 8

- Barcelona
- Transfer to the airport
- Flight to Kutaisi at 18:25
