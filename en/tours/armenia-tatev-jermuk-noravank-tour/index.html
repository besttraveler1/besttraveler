Armenia in 3 Days: Dilijan, Jermuk, Tatev, Noravank, Sevan

A three-day minibus tour to Armenia with two nights in the town of Vayk. A passport or ID card is needed to cross the border.

Tour programme

  1. Day 1

    • Departure from Tbilisi at 06:00, border procedures
    • Stop at a picnic spot near Alaverdi
    • Dilijan (free)
    • Haghartsin (free)
    • Goshavank (free)
    • Stop at a food place by Lake Sevan
    • Selim (Vardenyats) Pass
    • Vayk, check-in at the hotel

  2. Day 2

    • Jermuk waterfall (free)
    • Shaki waterfall (free)
    • Khndzoresk (free)
    • Tatev Monastery (free)
    • Vorotnavank (free)
    • Return to the hotel in Vayk

  3. Day 3

    • Noravank Monastery near Areni (free)
    • Selim (Vardenyats) Pass
    • Sevanavank Monastery (free)
    • Stop at a food place by Lake Sevan
    • Alaverdi, Tamar's Bridge
    • Return to Tbilisi

Included in the price

  • Travel by top-class minibus
  • Two nights in a hotel with breakfast
  • Services of a qualified tour guide (organization, short overview of each sight)
  • Photography

Optional extras

  • Boat trip on Lake Sevan — 10 GEL (optional)
  • Wings of Tatev cable car — 50 GEL one way (optional). It has only two stations, the upper one in Tatev and the lower one in Halidzor, and is listed in the Guinness Book of Records as the longest non-stop double-track cable car.

Good to know

  • Return: 23:00
  • Meeting point: Didube metro station, upper exit
  • Documents: International passport (valid for at least 6 months) or Georgian ID card
  • The tour runs in any weather.
