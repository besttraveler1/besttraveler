## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- Departure from Tbilisi at 06:00, border procedures
- Stop at a picnic spot near Alaverdi
- Dilijan (free)
- Haghartsin (free)
- Goshavank (free)
- Stop at a food place by Lake Sevan
- Selim (Vardenyats) Pass
- Vayk, check-in at the hotel

- 

### Day 2

- Jermuk waterfall (free)
- Shaki waterfall (free)
- Khndzoresk (free)
- Tatev Monastery (free)
- Vorotnavank (free)
- Return to the hotel in Vayk

- 

### Day 3

- Noravank Monastery near Areni (free)
- Selim (Vardenyats) Pass
- Sevanavank Monastery (free)
- Stop at a food place by Lake Sevan
- Alaverdi, Tamar's Bridge
- Return to Tbilisi
