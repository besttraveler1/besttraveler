India Group Tour: Rajasthan, Delhi, Jaisalmer and Udaipur

Tour programme

  1. Day 1: Tbilisi to Delhi and Mandawa

    • Flight from Tbilisi on 25 October at 23:45, arrival in Delhi on 26 October at 07:05
    • Transfer to the hotel; after breakfast, drive to Mandawa
    • Mandawa havelis, houses once owned by wealthy merchants
    • Breakfast, dinner and overnight at the hotel

  2. Day 2: Mandawa to Bikaner

    • Binsidhar Newatia Haveli, known for its bright, lively paintings
    • Gulab Rai Ladia Haveli, Goenka Double Haveli, Murmuria Haveli, Jhunjhunwala Haveli
    • Drive to Bikaner: Junagarh Fort, decorated with gold leaf, glass, mirror work and Dutch tiles
    • Breakfast, dinner and overnight at the hotel

  3. Day 3: Bikaner to Jaisalmer

    • Jaisalmer Fort, standing about 100 metres high
    • Havelis with carved facades and windows
    • Salim Singh Haveli (17th century), known for its peacock carvings
    • Nathumal Haveli (1885) and Patwon Haveli (1805)
    • Dinner and overnight at the hotel

  4. Day 4: Jaisalmer to Jodhpur

    • Drive to Jodhpur, hotel check-in and free time
    • Mehrangarh Fort on a 125-metre hill, one of the most impressive buildings in Rajasthan
    • Jaswant Thada, a white marble memorial (1899) to Maharaja Jaswant Singh II
    • Evening: the Clock Tower and Sardar Bazaar, with textiles, antiques, silverware and handicrafts
    • Breakfast and overnight at the hotel

  5. Day 5: Jodhpur to Udaipur via Ranakpur

    • Ranakpur temples (15th century), famous for their intricate architecture
    • Arrival in Udaipur, transfer to the hotel
    • Breakfast and overnight at the hotel

  6. Day 6: Udaipur, the City of Lakes

    • City Palace, the former home of the Mewar rulers, now mostly a museum overlooking Lake Pichola
    • Jagdish Mandir
    • Sahelion ki Bari
    • Bharatiya Lok Kala Mandal
    • Evening boat ride on Lake Pichola
    • Breakfast and overnight at the hotel

  7. Day 7: Udaipur to Pushkar

    • Brahma Temple and the ghats
    • Pushkar, a town on the shore of Pushkar Lake
    • Breakfast and overnight at the hotel

  8. Day 8: Pushkar to Delhi

    • After breakfast, drive to Delhi
    • Free time

  9. Day 9: Delhi to Tbilisi

    • Qutub Minar
    • India Gate
    • President's House and Parliament
    • Bangla Sahib
    • Agrasen ki Baoli, a historic stepwell 60 m long and 15 m wide
    • Dinner at Lazeez restaurant, then transfer to the international airport
