## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- Flight from Tbilisi (or Batumi) airport
- Transfer in Istanbul, hotel check-in, free time
- Turkish night and a Bosphorus cruise
- Dinner

- 

### Day 2

- 07:30 breakfast, 08:30 start of the tour
- Topkapi Palace and Harem
- Hagia Irene
- Sultan Ahmed Mosque (Blue Mosque)
- Ibrahim Pasha Palace
- Hurrem Sultan Hamam
- Lunch at a Turkish restaurant
- Boat trip: the Golden Horn, the Bosphorus, Rumeli Hisari fortress, Maiden's Tower
- Beylerbeyi Park

- 

### Day 3

- 07:30 breakfast
- Cruise to the Princes' Islands: Buyukada and Burgazada
- Lunch
- Leather showroom

- 

### Day 4

- Trip to Sile
- Lunch

- 

### Day 5

- 07:30 breakfast
- Basilica Cistern, dating from 532, from the time of old Constantinople
- Sultanahmet Square
- Hagia Sophia, the main church of old Constantinople
- Taksim Square and Istiklal Street
- Galata Tower

- 

### Day 6

- 07:30 breakfast
- St George's Cathedral and the Ecumenical Patriarchate
- The 'One-Day Church', where you can make a wish
- Valens Aqueduct
- Suleymaniye Mosque
- Balat, with its colourful houses
- Emirgan Park
- Spice Bazaar and Grand Bazaar: free time for shopping
- Transfer to the airport, flight back to Tbilisi (or Batumi)
