Racha 2-Day Tour: Tskhrajvari, Shaori, Nikortsminda, Utsera, Ghebi

A two-day minibus tour from Tbilisi to Racha. We leave on Saturday at 07:00 and return on Sunday at 23:00.

Tour programme

  1. Day 1

    • Tskhrajvari (walk, 0.9 km one way)
    • Shaori reservoir
    • Nikortsminda
    • Spring of Love (Kvabtkari)
    • Barakoni
    • Mravaldzali village and St George church (2000 m above sea level)

  2. Day 2

    • Utsera resort (the narrow 'hair' bridge and a shrine)
    • Chiora village (St George church and the historic Kviratskhoveli church of Chiora)
    • Gona, an abandoned village (by local transport)
    • Ghebi village
    • Oni synagogue

Good to know
