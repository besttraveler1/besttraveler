## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- 05:00 departure from Tbilisi
- Kartsakhi border crossing
- Kajis Tsikhe, a 10th-century fortress
- Keles Droi waterfall
- Kars Castle (1153)
- Kars, hotel check-in, free time

- 

### Day 2

- Ani Cathedral (built 989-1001)
- Ani archaeological site
- Ishak Pasha Palace (1784)
- Khtskonk Monastery
- Van, hotel check-in, free time

- 

### Day 3

- Van Fortress (9th-7th century BC)
- Akdamar Island
- Muradiye Waterfall
- Return to Tbilisi
