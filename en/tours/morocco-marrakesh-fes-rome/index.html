Tour programme

  1. Day 1

    • Arrival in Rome at 17:30
    • Hotel check-in

  2. Day 2

    • Trevi Fountain
    • Aelian Bridge (Ponte Sant'Angelo)
    • St. Peter's Basilica
    • Transfer to the airport at 18:30
    • Arrival in Marrakesh at 22:10, transfer to the hotel (15–20 min)

  3. Day 3

    • Marrakesh tour: Majorelle Garden, Yves Saint Laurent Museum, the medina and souks
    • Night in Marrakesh

  4. Day 4

    • Trip to Ait Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate with a visit to Atlas Film Studios (3.5 hours one way)
    • Night in Marrakesh

  5. Day 5

    • Transfer to Rabat (4 hours)
    • City tour and night in Rabat

  6. Day 6

    • Transfer to Chefchaouen (4 hours)
    • City tour and night in Chefchaouen

  7. Day 7

    • Chefchaouen – Volubilis – Meknes – Fes (4.5 hours)
    • Night in Fes

  8. Day 8

    • Fes city tour
    • Transfer to Casablanca (3.5 hours)
    • Night in Casablanca

  9. Day 9

    • Casablanca tour with a visit to the Hassan II Mosque
    • Free time
    • 17:00 — transfer to Marrakesh (3 hours)
    • Flight from Marrakesh at 22:45
    • Arrival at Fiumicino at 02:15
    • Hotel check-in (Fiumicino)

  10. Day 10

    • Free time in Fiumicino
    • Transfer to the airport
    • Flight to Kutaisi at 18:10
