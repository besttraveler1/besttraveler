## Tour programme

- 

### Day 1

- 06:00 departure from Tbilisi (via Sarpi) to Amasya
- Hotel check-in, free time, evening walk in the city

- 

### Day 2

- Amasya city
- Rock tombs of the Pontic kings
- Amasya Castle
- Avanos, an ancient town of craftsmen
- Monks Valley, with formations of volcanic origin
- Hotel check-in

- 

### Day 3

- Hot-air balloon flight (optional), or photos with the balloons in the background
- Goreme Open-Air Museum (10th-15th century churches)
- Silver shop (souvenir market), camel ride
- Tokali Church, with a unique fresco that is the prototype of the icon 'Thou Art the Vine'
- Uchisar Castle
- Pigeon Valley
- Turkish Night (optional, 30 EUR)

- 

### Day 4

- Pasabag (Queens' Gardens): the cell of St Simeon Stylites and multi-headed fairy chimneys; the place where vines were first grown in Anatolia
- Zelve, the Valley of the Monks
- House of St George's mother (where, by tradition, St George lived)
- St Nino's village (Ortahisar Castle)
- Saruhan Caravanserai (1249)
- Pancarlik Church, one of the longest church interiors in the world

- 

### Day 5

- Kayseri Castle, built in antiquity
- Kayseri bazaar (optional)
- Gumusler Monastery
- Ordu, hotel check-in
- Ordu cable car (optional), with panoramic views of the city, the Black Sea and Boztepe hill

- 

### Day 6

- Trabzon
- Little Hagia Sophia
- Shopping centre (optional)
- Return to Tbilisi
